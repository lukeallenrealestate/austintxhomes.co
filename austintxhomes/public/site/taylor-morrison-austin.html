The Taylor Morrison Home Funding consideration

TMHF is Taylor Morrison's in-house mortgage shop. Financing through TMHF unlocks the Taylor Morrison preferred-lender package, which typically combines closing credits with a rate buydown. The decision whether to take it rides on one comparison: does TMHF's quoted rate beat (or at least match) what an independent Austin lender quotes the same week? When TMHF is competitive, the incentive is pure net benefit. When TMHF prices 0.25-0.75 points above market, the 30-year amortization cost of that rate differential routinely wipes out the headline incentive and then some, costing $10K-$40K of lifetime loan interest. The comparison is a 48-hour pre-contract step most unrepresented buyers skip.

What is the Taylor Morrison Esplanade brand?

Taylor Morrison's 55-plus active adult community brand. Age-restricted standards with amenity packages for active retirees: fitness, pool, pickleball, tennis, resident clubs, senior infrastructure. Austin presence in specific master-planned sections rather than community-sized developments. Specific availability shifts over time.

What are typical Taylor Morrison Austin incentives?

Closing cost credits ($5K-$20K depending on home price), preferred lender rate buydown through TMHF, design studio allowance (less flexible than mid-tier builders), appliance package, extended rate lock on to-be-built. Standing inventory in slower-selling phases carries more aggressive stacks than fast-moving to-be-built.

What is Taylor Morrison Home Funding?

Taylor Morrison's affiliated mortgage lender. Users typically qualify for preferred lender incentive package (closing cost credits + rate buydown). Specific benefit varies. Buyer-side consideration: TMHF rate must be verified against broader market before contract to confirm the incentive produces net benefit versus independent lender with lower rate.
