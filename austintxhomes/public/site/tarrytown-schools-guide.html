Public Schools · Austin ISD

The Tarrytown Feeder
Pattern

Most Tarrytown addresses belong to Austin ISD and follow the same feeder pattern: Casis Elementary → O. Henry Middle School → Austin High School. This is the pattern that drives so much of the Tarrytown home-price premium — but "most" is not "all," and getting the specific school assignment right for a specific address is a step Luke Allen verifies on every Tarrytown property before an offer.

Elementary · Austin ISD
Casis Elementary
2710 Exposition Blvd · K to 5 · ~700 students

What it is. Casis Elementary sits on the Exposition Boulevard corridor at the heart of Tarrytown and is consistently rated among the top public elementary schools in Austin ISD. Test scores, parent satisfaction, and community engagement are all near the top of the district. It is the school families move to Tarrytown for.

Who attends. Casis draws from the core of Tarrytown and adjacent Bryker Woods pockets. Class sizes are typical Austin ISD (18 to 22). The parent community is highly engaged, with an active PTA and consistent fundraising for enrichment programs.

What buyers should know. Not every 78703 address feeds into Casis. Some Tarrytown addresses (particularly southern and eastern edges) feed into Matthews Elementary. Do not assume — verify. The AISD attendance boundary map is the source of truth; Zillow's school listings are frequently outdated.

Middle · Austin ISD
O. Henry Middle School
2610 W. 10th St · Grades 6 to 8 · Casis feeder

What it is. O. Henry Middle School serves the middle-school years for most Tarrytown Casis graduates. Located just east of the neighborhood in the West Enfield area, O. Henry has strong core academics, a solid arts program, and one of the more consistent athletic reputations among Austin ISD middle schools.

Alternative pathways. Some Tarrytown-area addresses feed into Lamar Middle School instead. High-performing Casis students can also test into Kealing Middle School's Magnet program (a highly competitive AISD-wide magnet at 1607 Pennsylvania Avenue). Luke Allen has clients who have taken each pathway.

Physical campus. O. Henry is a historic Austin campus, well-maintained, with recent updates to core facilities. Bus and car-line access is straightforward from most Tarrytown streets.

High School · Austin ISD
Austin High School
1715 W. Cesar Chavez St · Grades 9 to 12 · ~2,400 students

What it is. Stephen F. Austin High School is the flagship traditional high school of Austin ISD, sited on the shore of Lady Bird Lake just south of Tarrytown. It offers a full range of Advanced Placement courses, a well-established International Baccalaureate program, and one of the strongest fine arts programs in Central Texas. Austin High graduates place into UT Austin, Texas A&M, Rice, and out-of-state selective universities every year.

Athletics and activities. Austin High's athletic program competes in UIL Class 6A and covers the full range of varsity sports. The Fine Arts complex hosts theater, orchestra, band, and choir. Numerous student clubs and extracurricular options give the school a large-school feel with substantial breadth.

Transportation. From most of Tarrytown, Austin High is a 5- to 10-minute drive down Enfield or Lake Austin Boulevard. Bus service is provided.

The Eanes ISD Question

Western Tarrytown
and Eanes ISD

A small but important pocket of western Tarrytown falls within the boundaries of Eanes Independent School District rather than Austin ISD. Eanes is one of the highest-rated public school districts in Texas — often ranked in the top five statewide — and homes within the Eanes boundary carry a school premium of their own, separate from the Casis Elementary premium. If you are shopping the westernmost edge of Tarrytown, verify whether the address falls in AISD or Eanes ISD before you assume anything about which schools serve it.

Eanes ISD schools serving that pocket include Bridge Point Elementary, West Ridge Middle School, and Westlake High School. All three are highly rated. The tradeoff for families is real: Eanes-boundary Tarrytown addresses lose access to Casis and Austin High, but gain access to a district that consistently outperforms most in the state.

Verify the school zone before you make an offer.

The single most expensive assumption a Tarrytown buyer can make is that their target address feeds into Casis Elementary. It might not. Use the official Austin ISD address lookup at austinisd.org or the Eanes ISD boundary map at eanesisd.net. Or ask Luke Allen — he pulls it for every client, for free, before an offer is written.

Private Options

Private Schools Near
Tarrytown Austin

Tarrytown is one of the strongest private-school-adjacent neighborhoods in Austin. Multiple top-rated private schools are within a 15-minute drive, and several are essentially at the neighborhood's doorstep. Families who prefer independent, religious, or classical education have real options without leaving Tarrytown proper.

Independent · K to 12
St. Andrew's Episcopal School
On the western edge of Tarrytown along Balcones Drive. One of the top-rated K-12 independent schools in Texas. Strong academics, well-established athletic and arts programs, and a beautiful multi-campus setting.
Catholic · Grades 9 to 12
St. Michael's Catholic Academy
College-preparatory Catholic high school in central Austin, roughly a 10-minute drive from Tarrytown. Strong academics, active service programs, and consistent college placement.
Classical Christian · K to 12
Regents School of Austin
Classical Christian K-12 that draws heavily from Tarrytown and other central Austin neighborhoods. Rigorous academics with a distinctive classical curriculum framework.
Episcopal · K to 8

Small independent K-8 near downtown Austin. Highly regarded academics with a family-community feel that many Tarrytown families gravitate toward.
Progressive · K to 8

Progressive independent school with multiple campuses in central Austin. Draws Tarrytown families interested in Montessori-influenced and experiential education approaches.
Catholic · K to 8

Small parish-affiliated Catholic elementary near UT Austin. Convenient for Tarrytown families who prefer a Catholic elementary environment with strong core academics.

Common Questions

Tarrytown Schools,
FAQ

Is Casis Elementary really that good?
By the measurable standards — state test scores, parent-satisfaction survey data, and demographic outcomes — Casis is consistently one of the top-performing public elementaries in Austin ISD. Anecdotally, it also has strong community engagement, an active PTA, and long institutional continuity. That said, "best" is subjective; other AISD elementaries (Bryker Woods, Highland Park) also perform well.
Can I transfer into Casis if I don't live in-zone?
Austin ISD's out-of-zone transfer policy is limited. Casis rarely accepts transfers because it is at or near capacity most years. The reliable path in is to live in-zone. That is one of the reasons Tarrytown addresses inside the Casis boundary command a home-price premium.
Which Tarrytown addresses feed into Matthews Elementary instead?
Generally, southern and eastern-edge Tarrytown parcels feed into Matthews Elementary rather than Casis. The precise boundary shifts slightly year to year and needs to be checked address by address using the AISD lookup. Matthews is a strong school in its own right, but families who move to Tarrytown expecting Casis need to confirm before making an offer.
What about Kealing Middle School Magnet?
Kealing's Liberal Arts and Sciences Academy magnet is a highly competitive AISD-wide magnet middle school. Students apply in 5th grade and are admitted by test score. Many high-performing Casis graduates apply. Admission is not guaranteed. Kealing is an alternative to O. Henry for accepted students.
Is Austin High School good?
Austin High is the flagship AISD traditional high school. It offers a full AP catalog, an IB program, and 6A UIL athletics. Test scores and college placement are strong. Like all large urban high schools, outcomes depend heavily on student engagement — Austin High offers depth for motivated students and adequate options for the average student.
How does Westlake High School compare?
Westlake High (Eanes ISD) consistently ranks near the top of Texas public high schools by test scores and college placement. It is a smaller, more suburban campus than Austin High. Both are strong; the choice usually comes down to which neighborhood you live in rather than a school-by-school comparison. Tarrytown parcels in the Eanes boundary feed to Westlake.
Do Tarrytown kids walk to school?
Many do walk to Casis Elementary from surrounding blocks. Distance and family comfort vary; walking is realistic for parcels within roughly a 10-minute walk of the campus. Middle and high school students typically take the bus or get dropped off.
Does the school premium justify the home price?
For families with school-age children targeting a strong public school, the Casis premium generally holds its value at resale — the demand pool is deep and structural. For buyers without children or with older children, paying the premium purely for schools may not make financial sense; alternative Tarrytown blocks feeding into other AISD schools may offer better value.
