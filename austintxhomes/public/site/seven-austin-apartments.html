The Building

West 7th Street
Downtown Living

Seven sits at 615 West 7th Street in downtown Austin's Market District. This is west of Congress Avenue, walking distance to the Central Library, the Warehouse District, and the flagship Whole Foods. It's one of the more genuinely central downtown addresses you can rent at without paying the Rainey Street tower premium.

The floor plan mix is 1 bedroom and 2 bedroom only, with 27 distinct 1BR layouts and 9 distinct 2BR layouts. Named plans include The Bremond (both flat and loft variants), The Tower, and several standard 1BR configurations. Starting rents range from about $2,100 for a compact 1BR to $3,800 and up for a loft or 2BR.

Seven is professionally managed by Greystar, so pricing uses their two-column format: Base Rent for the apartment plus a Total Monthly Leasing Price that includes fixed mandatory monthly fees. When comparing Seven to other downtown buildings, always compare Total Monthly Leasing Price to Total Monthly Leasing Price, not base rent to base rent.

Market District
Downtown Austin

Seven's Market District location is one of the most walkable in downtown Austin. You're inside the CBD but on the calmer, more residential-feeling west side of Congress. Central Library and Republic Square Park are your neighborhood parks. Warehouse District bars and restaurants are 5 minutes on foot.
