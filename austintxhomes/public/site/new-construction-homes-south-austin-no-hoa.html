Where No-HOA New Construction Lives

Where True No-HOA New Construction Actually Exists

The reason "new construction with no HOA in South Austin" is a specific product type (not a filter on Zillow) is that master-planned suburban subdivisions and mid-density condo/townhome projects almost always carry HOAs. True no-HOA new construction is a narrow slice, mostly custom spec builders working on individual infill lots. Here is the honest map.

78704 Infill Teardowns and Custom Spec: The Prime No-HOA Zone

Custom spec builders (Rockit, LGI, StudioMET Architects, and independent builders) are actively building on individual teardown lots in Bouldin Creek, Zilker, Travis Heights, Barton Hills, and the South Lamar corridor. These lots were platted decades ago with no HOA overlay. New construction on these lots inherits the no-HOA status of the original parcel. Price: $1.2M to $3.5M. This is the highest-concentration no-HOA new construction pool in South Austin. See the guide to modern pool homes in 78704.

78745 Infill: The Middle-Budget No-HOA Option

78745 has a large stock of 1960s to 1980s single-family homes that are being torn down for new construction on the original individual lots. New construction in 78745 typically runs $550,000 to $950,000 for a 1,800 to 2,600 sqft build. Lots are generally 6,000 to 8,000 sqft. Most 78745 infill teardowns are no-HOA (they inherit the original neighborhood status). Newer master-planned areas within 78745 (some Southampton and Cherry Creek pockets) may have HOAs; verify address by address.

78748 Split: Custom Infill vs. Master-Planned Subdivisions

78748 is split into two very different product types. The northern half of 78748 has older neighborhoods with individual custom lots (mostly no-HOA infill new construction, $500K to $850K). The southern half of 78748 has newer master-planned subdivisions (Circle C, Onion Creek, Southpark Meadows) which almost universally have HOAs and often MUDs. Verify address before assuming HOA status.

Where HOAs Are Effectively Guaranteed

Master-planned suburban subdivisions built after approximately 1995 in South Austin (outer 78748, some 78745 pockets, Southpark Meadows, and virtually all of Manchaca ETJ and Buda) essentially always have HOAs, sometimes multiple layers (community HOA + neighborhood sub-HOA). If your budget forces you into these subdivisions, "no HOA new construction" is unlikely to exist as a real product.

The Condo and Townhome Reality

New construction condo and townhome projects across South Austin (Bouldin Creek, Zilker, East Riverside) universally have HOAs. If you want "detached, new construction, no HOA," you are effectively looking at single-family houses on individual lots only. That narrows the pool but clarifies the search.

How to Verify No-HOA Status

MLS descriptions are not authoritative on HOA status. Verify via: (1) the seller's disclosure form (Section 5 asks about HOAs directly), (2) the deed restrictions filed with the Travis County Clerk (some restrictions are private with dues; some are HOA-managed), (3) title commitment schedule B exceptions, and (4) direct contact with the builder. Every one of these should be checked before offer, not after option period.

The Trade-Offs of No-HOA New Construction

No-HOA new construction advantages: no monthly dues (typical Austin HOA: $50 to $400/month), no restrictions on exterior colors, landscaping, parking, and fencing, no assessment risk. Disadvantages: no shared amenities (pool, gym, park), no rulebook against neighbor eyesores, potentially less consistent neighborhood aesthetics. Most infill 78704 buyers actively prefer no-HOA. Most master-planned suburban buyers actively prefer HOA structure. Neither is objectively better.

Builder-Specific Context

Active South Austin custom spec builders in the no-HOA new construction pool include: Rockit Homes (Bouldin, Zilker, mid-price modern), LGI Homes (mid-market South Austin, some suburban HOA product also), StudioMET Architects (high-end 78704 custom modern), and dozens of smaller independent builders. Builder reputation, warranty structure, permit compliance history, and workmanship vary meaningfully. Luke has direct relationships with several of these builders and can flag reputation issues before you tour.

No-HOA South Austin New Construction Questions Answered

Can I actually find new construction homes in South Austin with no HOA?
Yes, in specific sub-markets. Infill teardowns and custom spec builds in 78704 (Bouldin Creek, Zilker, Travis Heights, Barton Hills, South Lamar corridor) are the highest-concentration no-HOA new construction area. 78745 infill teardowns and northern 78748 individual lots are the middle-budget option. Master-planned suburban subdivisions almost always have HOAs.
What do new construction homes in South Austin with no HOA cost?
True no-HOA new construction in 78704 core (Bouldin Creek, Zilker, Travis Heights, Barton Hills) trades $1.2M to $3.5M. 78745 infill teardown new construction runs $550,000 to $950,000 for 1,800 to 2,600 sqft. Northern 78748 individual-lot new construction runs $500,000 to $850,000. Southern 78748 master-planned new construction ($400,000 to $650,000) almost always has HOAs.
Which South Austin ZIPs have the most no-HOA new construction?
78704 (Bouldin Creek, Zilker, Travis Heights, Barton Hills, South Lamar corridor) has the highest concentration of no-HOA new construction because most parcels were platted decades before HOA structures became standard. 78745 has meaningful no-HOA infill teardown activity. Northern 78748 has some individual-lot no-HOA new construction. Master-planned subdivisions (outer 78748, some 78745 pockets) universally carry HOAs.
How do I verify no-HOA status before offer?
Four sources, verified in order: (1) seller's disclosure form Section 5 (asks about HOAs directly), (2) deed restrictions filed with the Travis County Clerk (some restrictions are private with dues; some are HOA-managed), (3) title commitment schedule B exceptions, and (4) direct contact with the builder. MLS listing descriptions are not authoritative. Luke pulls all four for every serious buyer target.
Are there advantages to no-HOA new construction?
No monthly dues (typical Austin HOA runs $50 to $400/month, some MUD subdivisions higher). No restrictions on exterior colors, landscaping, parking (RV, boat, work truck), or fencing. No special assessment risk. No approval bottleneck for exterior modifications. Downsides: no shared amenities (pool, gym, park), no rulebook against neighbor eyesores, potentially less consistent neighborhood aesthetics.
Which South Austin builders are actively building no-HOA new construction?
Active custom spec builders in the South Austin no-HOA new construction pool include Rockit Homes (Bouldin, Zilker, mid-price modern), LGI Homes (mid-market, mixed HOA/no-HOA), StudioMET Architects (high-end 78704 custom modern), and several independent builders. Reputation, warranty structure, and workmanship vary meaningfully. Luke has direct relationships with several of these builders.
Do condos or townhomes ever qualify as "no HOA new construction"?
Almost never. New construction condo and townhome projects across South Austin (Bouldin Creek, Zilker, East Riverside) universally have HOAs because the shared building envelope, roof, insurance, and common areas require an HOA structure by law. If you want "detached, new construction, no HOA," you are effectively looking at single-family houses on individual lots only.
Should I prefer no-HOA new construction over HOA new construction?
Depends on your priorities. No-HOA advantages: cost savings, freedom, no assessment risk. HOA advantages: amenities, consistent neighborhood aesthetics, dispute resolution structure. Most buyers targeting 78704 infill actively prefer no-HOA. Most suburban master-planned buyers actively prefer the HOA structure. Neither is objectively better.
