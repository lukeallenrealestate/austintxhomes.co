Pre-Listing Strategy

Renovate Before Selling
or Sell As-Is in Dripping Springs?

The honest answer depends on your subdivision, your specific home, and current buyer demand. Here are the two scenarios most Dripping Springs sellers face.

Sell As-Is
Best for tired homes in seller-favored price bands
Sub-$700K Dripping Springs homes remain in seller-favored territory with short days on market. If your home is dated but functional, selling as-is and pricing competitively often nets more than the same home would after a $40K-$80K cosmetic renovation. The buyer pool at this price point is largely young families and first-time DSISD families who will renovate to their own taste anyway. Save the money. Price honestly. Sell faster.
Renovate First
Best for $900K+ homes competing against new construction
At $900K and above, your Dripping Springs home competes directly with new construction in Belterra, Caliterra, and Headwaters. Dated kitchens and bathrooms in this price band substantially discount the home against the new-construction alternative. Targeted kitchen + primary bath renovation typically returns $1.40-$1.80 per dollar invested at this tier. Renovate strategically. Position against the new-build comparison. Net more.

Common Questions

Dripping Springs Seller FAQ

How much can I sell my Dripping Springs home for?
Values vary dramatically by subdivision, lot size, and DSISD feeder. Belterra typically $500K-$1.2M. Caliterra $550K-$1.4M. Headwaters $700K-$2M. Howard Ranch $900K-$3M+. La Ventana $1M-$5M+. Luke Allen provides honest comp-driven valuations within 24-48 hours - subdivision-level analysis, not Zillow estimates.
Should I update my Dripping Springs home before selling?
It depends. Kitchens and bathrooms typically pay back. Roof, HVAC, well/septic repairs are usually mandatory. Cosmetic paint and flooring often pay back. Pool additions, structural additions, and major landscaping rarely return cost. Luke Allen evaluates every potential investment against actual comparable sales.
What is the best time to sell a Dripping Springs home?
Spring (March-May) is peak activity, particularly for DSISD-anchored homes. Summer stays strong for relocations. Fall and winter are quieter but eliminate competing inventory. Acreage and luxury timing is more flexible because the buyer pool is smaller and less seasonal.
How long does it take to sell in Dripping Springs?
Varies by price band. Sub-$700K typically 30-60 days. $1M-$1.5M 60-120 days. $1.5M+ acreage and luxury 120-300+ days. Pricing strategy matters enormously - homes priced 5-8% above realistic comps sit and ultimately sell for less than they would have at a competitive ask.

Should I sell on my own (FSBO) in Dripping Springs?
FSBO sellers typically net 5-15% less than agent-listed sales in Dripping Springs.
