The 78704 Seller Playbook

What Actually Matters When Selling in 78704

Selling a house in 78704 is not the same as selling anywhere else in Austin. Sub-neighborhood dynamics vary meaningfully. Buyer profiles vary. Pricing psychology varies. Here is the honest playbook for how 78704 homes actually sell in 2026, and what a seller needs to get right.

Get the Sub-Neighborhood Pricing Right

78704 is not a single market. Bouldin Creek (37 active listings) and Travis Heights (25 active) command different premiums than Zilker and Barton Hills. Galindo, Dawson, and the South First corridor each have their own comparable-sales cluster. Pricing to a metro-wide average or a broad "78704 median" almost always underprices premium sub-neighborhoods and overprices value ones. Luke pulls sub-neighborhood-specific comparable sales for every 78704 listing before setting the list price.

Understand the 78704 Buyer Profile

The 78704 buyer in 2026 is a specific person: tech-employed household with a downtown-adjacent commute preference, valuing walkability, Central Austin amenities (Barton Springs, Zilker Park, South Congress restaurants), and Austin ISD schools (mostly Barton Hills, Zilker, or Becker feeder). Your listing photos, staging, and marketing copy need to speak to this specific buyer. A home marketed as generic "South Austin" underperforms a home marketed with 78704-specific lifestyle language.

Price for the Current 78704 Median, Not the 2022 Peak

The 78704 median list price is currently around $850,000 for detached SFR and $375,000 to $650,000 for condos, depending on tier. The 2022 peak was materially higher. Overpricing your 78704 home to 2022 numbers is the single most common seller mistake. Well-priced 78704 homes sell in 25 to 45 days. Overpriced homes sit for 90+ days, reduce, and eventually close 5% to 12% below their original list. Discipline on day-one pricing is worth tens of thousands.

Pre-List Prep Is a Real Number

A 78704 home that shows well can sell for $30,000 to $80,000 more than the same home shown poorly. Pre-list prep matters: paint touch-ups, professional cleaning, decluttering, minor repairs, and sometimes selective staging. Luke walks through your home before listing and gives you a specific pre-list prep plan that maximizes net proceeds without over-investing. Not every home needs $10,000 of pre-list work; some need $2,000 that returns $60,000.

The Marketing Plan Goes Beyond MLS

MLS syndication (Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin) is table stakes. Real 78704 marketing includes: professional interior + drone photography (~$400 to $700), a walk-through video, custom copy that leads with lifestyle (not just square footage), targeted outreach to the buyer-agent networks that transact most frequently in 78704, and social distribution. Every 78704 listing Luke represents gets the full package.

The Timing Question

The 78704 seller peak-activity window historically runs February through May (spring buyer influx from downtown-tech relocation cycles) and again in September through mid-November (fall pre-holiday closing push). Summer (June through August) sees Central Austin seller activity slow because families have already moved. Winter (December through mid-January) is thin. That said: well-priced 78704 homes sell in any month. Timing matters less than pricing discipline.

The Big-Team vs. Direct-Agent Choice

When interviewing agents to list your 78704 home, you will hear from both large-team brokerages (with celebrity-agent branding, extensive marketing budgets, and team hand-offs) and direct-service boutique agents (like Luke) who work with you personally through the entire transaction. Interview both, ask specific 78704-track-record questions, and choose based on what matters most for your specific home.
