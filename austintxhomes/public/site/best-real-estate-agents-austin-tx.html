Austin Market Context

The Austin Real Estate Market Right Now

The Austin metro market in 2026 has decelerated meaningfully from its 2021–2022 peak. Metro-wide active inventory sits at approximately 22,000 for-sale listings — the highest in a decade — and the median list price hovers near $470,000 across the full metro. Days on market has climbed to 60+ in most submarkets; 100+ in Central Austin luxury tiers. Price reductions on active listings are running at roughly 47% metro-wide.

This is the most buyer-favorable Austin market since 2018. But that surface reading masks enormous submarket variation. Hyde Park single-family homes are still transacting at 100% of list when priced right. Tarrytown's median active list is $2.35M with a +40% pool premium. Westlake's median is $3.15M with lots averaging 0.629 acres. Mueller's condo market operates on completely different dynamics than its townhouse or SFR markets. A good Austin agent knows which of these submarket realities applies to YOUR specific situation.
