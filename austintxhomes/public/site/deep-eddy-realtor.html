Luke's Take on Deep Eddy

Deep Eddy has real flood-risk considerations you don't have in the rest of 78703. Several blocks near Lake Austin Boulevard and the lower elevation streets have had flooding events. Any home purchase here should include a review of the specific address's FEMA flood zone status and any historical flood-related insurance claims on that property. It's not always a dealbreaker, but it changes insurance costs and financing options.

Deep Eddy sellers should know that their buyer pool skews toward people who already know the neighborhood — cyclists using the Butler Trail, Deep Eddy Pool members, longtime Central Austin residents who've wanted a Deep Eddy address for years. What is the median home price in Deep Eddy?

Small-lot cottages typically trade $900K to $1.6M. Larger or view-adjacent properties reach $2M+. Deep Eddy is generally more affordable per home than Tarrytown or Old Enfield because lots are smaller, though price per square foot can be similar.

Is Deep Eddy in a flood zone?

Portions of Deep Eddy near Lake Austin Boulevard and lower-elevation blocks fall in FEMA-designated flood zones. Any address should be individually verified through FEMA's flood map service and a review of the property's historical insurance claims. Flood-zone status affects insurance costs and some mortgage products.

What is the Deep Eddy Pool proximity premium?

Homes within a 5-minute walk of Deep Eddy Pool trade at a meaningful premium over otherwise-similar homes further away. Most of the neighborhood falls within that walking radius, which is part of why Deep Eddy pricing is more uniform than larger neighborhoods.

What schools serve Deep Eddy?

Mathews Elementary, O. Henry Middle, and Austin High. All Austin ISD. Deep Eddy is outside the Casis Elementary catchment (unlike Old Enfield and Pemberton Heights), which is one reason its home values run a bit below equivalent-condition Casis-zoned homes.

How does Deep Eddy compare to Tarrytown or Clarksville?

Deep Eddy is smaller, more compact, with tighter lots and a more casual character (cottages, cyclists, Deep Eddy Cabaret vibe). Tarrytown is larger lots and more established. Clarksville is more historic-district-restricted. Deep Eddy is the "affordable" corner of 78703, though "affordable" here still means $900K+.
