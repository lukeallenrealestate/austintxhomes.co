The Neighborhoods

The Eight Neighborhoods Apple Employees Actually Choose

Apple's Parmer Lane campus opened its Phase 1 in 2022 with room for approximately 15,000 total employees at full buildout. The neighborhoods below sit inside the 20-minute commute radius, offer stronger price value than the Domain-core condos or Westlake luxury tier, and have positive 2026 appreciation trajectories driven by employer concentration.

Bratton, 78727: Walkable to the Domain

Bratton sits south of the Apple campus (about 8 to 12 minutes drive) and is walkable to the Domain retail and restaurant corridor. Housing stock is mostly 1980s to 2000s SFR with some newer townhome infill. Median SFR price in 2026: $625,000 to $825,000. Appeals to Apple employees who want lifestyle amenities (Rock Rose district, walkable dining) plus a reasonable commute.

Avery Ranch, 78717: The Master-Planned Prestige Choice

Avery Ranch is the master-planned prestige neighborhood immediately north of the Apple campus (5 to 10 minutes drive). Golf course community, extensive amenities (multiple pools, tennis, walking trails), Round Rock ISD schools. Median SFR price: $700,000 to $1.2M. HOAs are meaningful ($100 to $250/month). This is the top-of-market Apple-corridor choice for families.

Ranch at Brushy Creek, 78717: Family-Focused, Newer

Ranch at Brushy Creek (also 78717, adjacent to Avery Ranch) is a slightly newer master-planned community with family-oriented amenities and highly rated Round Rock ISD schools. Median SFR: $650,000 to $950,000. Commute to Apple: 6 to 12 minutes. Trades at a slight discount to Avery Ranch with a similar amenity package.

Anderson Mill, 78729: The Value Play in Transition

Anderson Mill (78729, west of the Apple campus) is one of the strongest value plays in the Apple corridor. Established 1980s to 1990s SFR neighborhoods with mature landscaping. Prices are meaningfully lower: median $525,000 to $675,000. Round Rock ISD schools. Commute to Apple: 10 to 15 minutes. Buyers who prioritize value over amenity infrastructure choose this corridor. Some blocks are seeing meaningful renovation activity, driving comp appreciation.

Milwood, 78729: Family Value with Solid Schools

Milwood, adjacent to Anderson Mill in 78729, offers similar value with slightly newer housing stock. Median SFR: $550,000 to $700,000. Round Rock ISD schools. Family-safe streets, established neighborhood culture. Commute to Apple: 12 to 18 minutes. Strong choice for Apple employees wanting Round Rock ISD access at a lower price than Avery Ranch.

Villages of Anderson Mill, 78729: Newer Infill

Villages of Anderson Mill (78729) is the newer master-planned infill within the Anderson Mill corridor. Newer construction, HOA amenities, family-focused. Median SFR: $625,000 to $825,000. Commute to Apple: 12 to 16 minutes. Strong choice for buyers who want new-construction character with the Anderson Mill area's Round Rock ISD access.

Twin Creeks, 78681: North Round Rock Prestige

Twin Creeks (78681, Round Rock) is a highly amenity-rich master-planned community with a golf course, extensive walking trails, multiple pools, and Round Rock ISD access. Median SFR: $675,000 to $1.1M. Commute to Apple: 15 to 22 minutes. Chosen by Apple employees who prioritize amenity-community lifestyle and are willing to trade a slightly longer commute for it.

Behrens Ranch, 78681: Family-Focused Master-Planned

Behrens Ranch (78681) is the family-focused master-planned community in North Round Rock. Highly rated Round Rock ISD schools, playground and pool amenities, family-safe streets. Median SFR: $550,000 to $825,000. Commute to Apple: 18 to 25 minutes. Solid value tier for families prioritizing schools and community over commute optimization.

Frequently Asked

Apple Corridor Housing Questions Answered

What are the best upcoming neighborhoods near the Apple campus in North Austin?
Bratton (78727, walkable to Domain), Avery Ranch (78717, prestige master-planned), Ranch at Brushy Creek (78717, family-focused), Anderson Mill (78729, value play), Milwood (78729, family value), Villages of Anderson Mill (78729, newer infill), Twin Creeks (78681, North Round Rock prestige), and Behrens Ranch (78681, family master-planned). All are within a 5 to 25 minute drive of the Apple Parmer Lane campus.
Where is the Apple campus in North Austin?
Apple's Austin campus is at 6900 W Parmer Ln, Austin TX 78717, just west of Parmer Ln between US-183 and RM 620. Phase 1 opened in 2022. The site has room for approximately 15,000 total employees at full buildout, currently housing 6,000+ Apple employees. The Domain retail and restaurant corridor is 5 to 10 minutes south.
What is the median home price in the Apple corridor?
Median SFR prices across the eight Apple-corridor neighborhoods range from $525,000 (Anderson Mill entry) to $1.2M (Avery Ranch premium). Value-tier options (Anderson Mill, Milwood, entry Behrens Ranch): $525K to $700K. Mid-tier (Bratton, Ranch at Brushy Creek, Villages of Anderson Mill): $625K to $825K. Premium tier (Avery Ranch, Twin Creeks premium): $700K to $1.2M.
What is the commute from these neighborhoods to Apple?
Avery Ranch and Ranch at Brushy Creek (78717): 5 to 12 minutes drive. Bratton (78727): 8 to 12 minutes. Anderson Mill and Villages of Anderson Mill (78729): 10 to 16 minutes. Milwood (78729): 12 to 18 minutes. Twin Creeks (78681): 15 to 22 minutes. Behrens Ranch (78681): 18 to 25 minutes. Peak-hour times run 3 to 8 minutes longer.
Which Apple-corridor schools are best?
The Apple corridor is predominantly Round Rock ISD. Highly rated elementary schools include Anderson Mill Elementary, Great Oaks, Cactus Ranch, Chandler Oaks, and Redbud. All feed into Round Rock ISD middle and high schools which are consistently among the strongest suburban public districts in Texas. Some parts of 78727 fall in Austin ISD; verify address by address. See the Austin family neighborhoods guide for the broader comparison.
Are HOAs common in the Apple corridor?
Most Apple-corridor neighborhoods have HOAs. Avery Ranch, Ranch at Brushy Creek, Twin Creeks, Behrens Ranch, and Villages of Anderson Mill all carry HOAs ($100 to $250/month typical). Anderson Mill and Milwood have optional or no HOA in many pockets. Bratton varies by subdivision. If HOA-free is a priority, see the South Austin no-HOA new construction guide (South Austin, not North Austin, is where HOA-free new construction concentrates).
Which of these neighborhoods is best for Apple employees with families?
Avery Ranch and Ranch at Brushy Creek (78717) offer the strongest combination of top-rated Round Rock ISD schools, walkable amenities (pools, trails, tennis, playgrounds), and family-safe streets at 5 to 12 minutes from the Apple campus. Twin Creeks and Behrens Ranch (78681) are strong alternatives in Round Rock proper. Milwood (78729) is the best value option with strong schools and a longer but still reasonable commute.
Is the Apple corridor a good long-term investment?
Historically strong. Apple's continued campus expansion, the Domain corridor buildout, and the tech-employer concentration in the north Austin corridor create sustained demand for housing in the 15-minute commute radius. All eight neighborhoods have appreciated meaningfully over 10-year windows. Central Austin has appreciated faster, but at meaningfully higher entry prices. Individual outcomes vary by neighborhood, sub-market, and property.
