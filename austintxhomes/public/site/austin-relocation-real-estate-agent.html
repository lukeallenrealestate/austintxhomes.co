Can I buy an Austin home entirely remotely without visiting?
Yes. Luke has closed transactions for buyers who never visited Austin before closing. Requires: comprehensive virtual tours, remote inspection coordination, appraisal handling without buyer presence, and remote closing procedures (mail-away or notary services). Most buyers do a 2 to 3 day scout trip if possible, but it's not required for a well-run remote transaction.
What Austin neighborhoods work best for Tesla Gigafactory workers?
Tesla's Gigafactory is in Del Valle, southeast Austin. Best commute proximity: Del Valle itself, Manor, Elgin, Bastrop, and eastern portions of Pflugerville. For workers wanting more urban amenity, East Austin and southeast corridors (Riverside, Oltorf area) are viable with 20 to 25 minute commutes. See the Tesla relocation guide for detailed breakdown.
What Austin neighborhoods work best for Meta or Google employees?
Meta's primary Austin office is at The Domain (north Austin). Best commute proximity: Domain-adjacent apartments and condos, Anderson Mill, Great Hills, northwest Austin, and the Domain-Round-Rock-Cedar Park triangle. Google's Austin footprint spans downtown and The Domain, best options depend on which office you're assigned to. See Meta and Google relocation guides for specifics.
How is Texas real estate different from California / New York?
Major differences: (1) no state income tax on household income, (2) higher property taxes on your home (~2% of assessed value average, vs 0.7% CA, 1.4% NY), (3) different transaction structure, Texas uses title companies rather than closing attorneys, (4) homestead exemption filing required, (5) different contract standard forms (TREC forms), (6) higher homeowner insurance costs (hail/wind exposure). Luke walks every relocating buyer through Texas-specific differences BEFORE offer.
Should I rent first or buy immediately when relocating to Austin?
Depends on your certainty about the specific neighborhood and property. If confident, buying immediately avoids double moves. If uncertain, renting 6 to 12 months gives you time to experience Austin neighborhoods before committing. Many relocations use an intermediate approach: short-term corporate housing for 30 to 60 days, then closing on a home once you've identified the right neighborhood. Luke helps evaluate which structure fits your situation.
What Austin suburbs have the best schools for relocating families?
Depends on children's specific needs. Eanes ISD (Westlake) is Texas's highest-ranked public district. Lake Travis ISD, Leander ISD, Round Rock ISD also rank consistently high. Within Austin ISD: Casis (Tarrytown), Lee (Hyde Park), Bryker Woods (78703), Zilker Elementary, and Barton Hills Elementary all carry strong reputations. See Westlake / Eanes ISD, Tarrytown, and Barton Hills reports.
Does Luke work with employer relocation packages?
Yes. Luke coordinates with employer relocation coordinators (Sirva, Cartus, Weichert, Graebel) when applicable, handles specific vendor requirements, timeline expectations, and documentation your employer needs. If your employer offers a home purchase stipend or closing cost assistance, Luke helps structure the transaction to maximize what you can claim.
