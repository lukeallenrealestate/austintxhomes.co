Why the ceiling transfer matters so much for long-tenure Austin owners

Austin property values have risen substantially over the past 15-20 years. For a homeowner who established a 65-plus homestead ceiling in 2010-2015 on a home that has since doubled or tripled in appraised value, the ceiling benefit is often $8,000-$15,000 annually in school district tax savings versus what the owner would otherwise pay. When these owners downsize, they frequently assume the ceiling benefit disappears with the move. In most cases that assumption is wrong: Texas law allows the ceiling percentage to transfer to the new homestead with proper documentation.

The practical implication: a long-tenure Austin senior downsizing to Sun City Georgetown, Heritage at Vizcaya, or any other Texas homestead can preserve a significant portion of the ongoing tax benefit established over years in the current home. This can produce $3,000-$8,000 of annual tax savings on the downsized home compared to what the same home would cost a buyer without the transferred ceiling. Over a 10-20 year hold in the downsized home, that is $30,000-$160,000 in cumulative tax savings. Running this calculation before the downsize is one of the specific services Luke coordinates.

What is the Texas 65-plus homestead tax ceiling and how does it work?

Texas property tax provision that freezes the school district portion of homestead property tax the year the owner turns 65, provided an active homestead exemption is in place. Future school district taxes on that homestead cannot exceed the frozen amount even if assessed value rises. Many Texas counties and cities offer parallel 65-plus freezes on their portions. Significant benefit for long-tenure owners facing substantial appreciation.

Can I transfer my 65-plus tax ceiling if I downsize to a new Texas home?

Yes under specific conditions. Transfer is percentage-based, not dollar-based. The percentage your old home's school tax paid under the ceiling (vs no-ceiling calculation) applies to the new home's school tax. Requested through the receiving appraisal district with documentation. Can produce substantial ongoing tax savings most Austin realtors do not communicate.
