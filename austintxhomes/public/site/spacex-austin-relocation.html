Know Before You Search

SpaceX Starlink Is
in Bastrop, TX

Most of Austin's tech employers cluster north of the river - Apple, Dell, IBM, Samsung are all in the Domain or Round Rock corridor. SpaceX is different. The Starlink campus where satellites and user terminals are built sits on FM 1209 in Bastrop, southeast of Austin off TX-71. That means your daily commute runs east on TX-71 - the opposite direction of Austin's worst rush-hour traffic.

This matters enormously for your housing search. Neighborhoods popular with Austin tech workers - Cedar Park, Leander, northwest Austin - add 45-60 minutes to your commute compared to neighborhoods on the southeast side. Bastrop itself, Cedar Creek, Elgin, and East Austin are the realistic options. Most Zillow searches ignore this. I won't.

The First Decision

Should You Rent First
or Buy Right Away?

No universal answer - but here's the honest framework for SpaceX relocations specifically.

Rent First

Pros
  • Learn whether you want Bastrop's small-town pace or Austin's urban life
  • Flexibility if your role, team, or schedule shifts
  • No relocation stress stacked on top of new-job onboarding
Cons
  • Bastrop-area rentals are limited - inventory thinner than Austin proper
  • Building zero equity while Texas housing trends upward
  • Harder to settle kids in schools mid-year if you move twice
Best for
  • First time in Central Texas, no specific area in mind
  • First 6 to 12 months while you orient to the corridor
  • Buyers waiting for market conditions or specific inventory

Buy on Arrival

Pros
  • Start building equity immediately - every payment counts
  • Bastrop-area inventory turns fast - committed buyers move first
  • Stability for family and school enrollment
Cons
  • Need 30 to 45 days to close - start the search early
  • Requires knowing which neighborhood fits your commute and lifestyle
  • Less flexibility if the role doesn't work out
Best for
  • Relocating families who've done the neighborhood research
  • Buyers with a 3+ year commitment to SpaceX and Austin
  • Anyone with kids - avoids a disruptive mid-year school switch

The Financial Upside

What Texas Taxes Mean
for Your SpaceX Salary

The tax math is one of the most underappreciated parts of relocating to Texas from California or other high-tax states.

💰
State Income Tax
Zero. Texas Has None.

On a $140K SpaceX engineering salary, no state income tax means $10,000 to $14,000 more take-home per year vs. California. Over 5 years that's $50,000 to $70,000 - more than a down payment on a Bastrop or Elgin home. This is real money, not a rounding error.

🏠
Property Tax Reality
High - Budget for It

The offset: Texas property taxes run 1.8%, 2.4% of assessed value. Bastrop County rates are slightly lower than Travis County. On a $400K Bastrop home, budget $7,200 to $9,600/year ($600 to $800/mo). Factor this into your affordability calculation - lenders include it in your monthly payment. It's the most common surprise for California relocators.

📋
Relocation Timing
Use SpaceX's Package Wisely

SpaceX engineering relocation packages typically cover moving costs and may include a temporary housing stipend. If SpaceX is covering your first 30 to 60 days, use that window to rent short-term and buy with intention rather than rushing into the first available home. 60 days is enough time to close if you start the search before you arrive.

Before You Arrive

Austin Relocation
Checklist for SpaceX Employees

The practical tasks most relocators learn the hard way. Do these in order.

  • Get Texas driver's license within 90 days of establishing residency
  • Register vehicle in Texas (no annual inspection required post-2025)
  • Register to vote in Bastrop, Travis, or Williamson County
  • Open a local bank account - Frost Bank and Amplify Credit Union are Texas staples
  • Set up Bluebonnet Electric (Bastrop area) or Austin Energy (city) service
  • Research homestead exemption - file after Jan 1 of your first full year of ownership
  • Get pre-approved for a mortgage before your start date if you plan to buy
  • Research school district boundaries for target neighborhoods - address matters, not city name
  • Get a TxTag if commuting via SH-130 toll road from Pflugerville or Round Rock
  • Budget for summer electric bills - Central Texas AC runs May, October at $180 to $280/mo
  • Plan for Texas weather: hail insurance riders are common, ask about flood zone status

Common Questions

Where do most SpaceX Bastrop employees live?
The most common areas are Bastrop itself, Cedar Creek/Tahitian Village, East Austin, Del Valle, and Elgin. Bastrop and Cedar Creek offer the shortest commute (5 to 18 minutes) and the most affordable housing. East Austin and South Austin (78744/78745) give you a true Austin lifestyle with a 25 to 35 minute drive in. Elgin is a strong value play 18 to 28 minutes north of the campus.
How long is the commute from Austin to SpaceX Starlink Bastrop?
It depends heavily on where you live. Bastrop (in-town): 5 to 12 min. Cedar Creek: 10 to 18 min. Elgin: 18 to 28 min. Smithville: 18 to 25 min. Del Valle: 20 to 28 min. East Austin / Mueller: 28 to 38 min. South Austin: 25 to 35 min. Pflugerville via SH-130: 30 to 40 min. Avoid northwest Austin (Cedar Park, Leander, Domain area) - those commutes run 60 to 75 min each way.
Does SpaceX offer relocation assistance for the Bastrop campus?
SpaceX typically offers relocation packages for engineering and senior production hires. Packages commonly cover moving expenses and may include a temporary housing stipend or lump-sum allowance. Production technician relocation is more limited and varies by hiring need. Confirm your specific package with SpaceX HR or your recruiter - terms differ by role, level, and hire date.
Should I rent or buy when relocating for SpaceX Austin?
If you've never lived in Central Texas, renting for 6 to 12 months to learn the area is reasonable. If you're relocating with a family and have done your neighborhood research, buying immediately makes financial sense - you start building equity and avoid a second move. I can run the specific rent vs. buy math for your situation at no cost.
Is SpaceX Bastrop the same as SpaceX Starbase?
No. Starbase is in Boca Chica near Brownsville, on the Texas Gulf Coast - that's where Starship is built and launched. The SpaceX Bastrop facility (sometimes called the "Starlink campus") is in Central Texas, about 30 miles southeast of Austin, and produces Starlink satellites and user terminals. Most Austin-area SpaceX hiring happens for the Bastrop campus, not Starbase.
Are there good schools near SpaceX Bastrop?
Yes, across several districts. Bastrop is served by Bastrop ISD - solid mid-range, growing with new campuses. Cedar Creek by Bastrop ISD as well. Elgin by Elgin ISD - improving. Smithville by Smithville ISD - small district, tight community. For top-tier schools, some families extend the commute to Del Valle ISD, Austin ISD's stronger campuses, or Hays CISD (Kyle/Buda). Research campus-level ratings before choosing an address.
