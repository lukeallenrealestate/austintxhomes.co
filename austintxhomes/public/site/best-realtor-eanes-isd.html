The District

About Eanes ISD (78733 & 78746)

Eanes Independent School District is consistently ranked the top public school district in Texas by Niche.com and recognized by the Texas Education Agency as one of the highest-performing districts in the state. The district serves Westlake Hills, Rollingwood, and surrounding unincorporated areas of Travis County west of Austin, primarily within the 78733 and 78746 zip codes.

Westlake High School, the district's sole high school, regularly places among the top 100 public high schools in the United States according to U.S. News & World Report, with exceptional AP enrollment, near-100% college placement, and state-championship-level athletic and fine arts programs. Eanes ISD also operates two middle schools (Hill Country Middle and West Ridge Middle) and six elementary schools: Bridge Point, Cedar Creek, Eanes, Forest Trail, Valley View, and Barton Creek. Each elementary attendance zone has clearly defined boundaries that significantly affect resale value, Luke advises on these block-by-block before any offer is written.

The district is geographically compact, covering roughly 8 square miles. This means a tight-knit community, well-funded schools (driven by high property tax revenue from home values in the $900K to $8M+ range), and student-to-teacher ratios well below the Texas average. Property taxes inside Eanes ISD are also notably lower than equivalent Austin ISD addresses because the district is a non-AISD enclave with its own taxing structure.

For families relocating to Austin where school quality is the primary driver, Eanes ISD is typically the first and last district they consider.
