The Five Luxury Submarkets
Luke Allen Specializes In

Central Austin luxury concentrates in five distinct submarkets, each with different buyer pools, pricing dynamics, and negotiation patterns. Luke covers all five with the same subdivision-level depth demonstrated in the deep-dive market reports on this site.

Tarrytown

Tarrytown (78703), Historic Luxury

Median active list $2.35M ($769/sqft). Pemberton Heights Sec 08 at $6.95M median leads pricing; Westfield A at $458/sqft is the value play. Casis Elementary drives 78% of active inventory pricing. Pool premium +39.6% on $/sqft. See the full Tarrytown market report.

Westlake / Eanes ISD

Westlake / Eanes ISD ($3.15M median)

Median 0.629-acre lot (3.3x Tarrytown's median). 56% of inventory sits on 0.5+ acre lots. Pool premium +50.6%. Eanes ISD is Texas's highest-ranked public district. Rivercrest at $2,204/sqft leads pricing; Davenport, Camelback, and Lago Villa follow. See the full Westlake / Eanes ISD report.

Rob Roy / Cat Mountain

Rob Roy / Cat Mountain / Northwest Hills

Gated luxury communities on Austin's hill country ridges. Rob Roy specifically operates as a gated development with private roads and neighborhood covenants. Pricing typically $2M to $8M. Buyer pool skews executive relocations and privacy-focused Austin professionals. Inventory turnover slow, many owners hold 15+ years.

Barton Creek

Barton Creek, Golf, Estate, Country Club

Barton Creek Country Club with golf, resort amenities, and gated estate lots. Pricing $2M to $10M+. Buyer pool skews Austin-tenured executives and golf-oriented lifestyle buyers. Country club membership implications matter for transaction structure. Amarra, Escala, and Estancia are specific subdivisions within Barton Creek.

Lake Austin waterfront

Lake Austin Waterfront, Riverfront Estates

Direct Lake Austin waterfront properties in West Lake Hills, Rob Roy, Cat Mountain, and the Rivercrest / Davenport corridor. Pricing $3M to $20M+ depending on frontage length, boat dock condition, and elevation. LCRA permitting, dock covenants, and floodplain considerations material. Extremely thin inventory, often 5 to 15 listings at any time. Off-market activity substantial.

How much of Austin's luxury inventory is off-market?
Meaningful share, particularly at the top of the market ($4M+).
