Methodology

How to actually choose an Austin realtor.

Three variables matter more than review count. Review count is a lagging indicator of past volume, not a leading indicator of fit for your specific transaction.

1

Transaction-specific experience

A new construction buyer wants a realtor who tours builder sales offices every week. A 1031 buyer wants someone who tracks the exchange calendar. A luxury seller wants discretion. Match the realtor's specialty to your transaction, not their overall rating.

2

Response time on the first call

The realtor who replies personally within one business hour on the first inquiry is almost always the realtor who protects your offer deadline, inspection window, and close timeline later. Slow first reply predicts slow later work.

3

Live tool depth

Ask what the realtor will send you after the first call. A curated shortlist pulled from live MLS data that same day is a different signal than a saved-search email that recycles weekly. The first means they build; the second means they rely on canned flows.
