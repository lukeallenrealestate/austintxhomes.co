Buyer Strategy · 2026

How a Brentwood Buyer
Should Approach the 2026 Market

The 2026 Brentwood market is the most buyer-favorable in Central Austin: 61% reduction rate, average reduction 9.3%, 111-day median DOM, 18.6 months of supply. The structural drivers (Brentwood Elementary, Walk Score 78, Shipe Park, Burnet Road retail) are unchanged, the rebuild has actually IMPROVED neighborhood amenity. Six tactical recommendations:

01
Builder spec inventory is over-supplied
21 of 31 active SFR are new construction. The supply has run ahead of buyer absorption at this price tier, particularly $1.5M to $2M. Many spec homes are now 90+ days on market with the builder carrying construction debt. Offers 7 to 10% below ask are realistic for stale spec inventory. The trick is identifying which builders are over-leveraged vs. which can hold.
02
Original cottage stock is a vanishing asset
Only 9 active SFR (29%) are pre-1960 original Brentwood cottages, median list $599K. Without NCCD protection, this stock continues to teardown each year. For buyers who specifically want a Brentwood cottage with character, small lots, mature trees, walkable proximity to Shipe Park, 2026 is the year of best selection at sub-$700K entry. By 2028, that supply may be half what it is today.
03
The single-feeder AISD simplicity
Brentwood's AISD path is clean: Brentwood Elementary → Lamar Middle → McCallum (or McCallum Fine Arts Academy). No boundary splits, no charter ambiguity. For families with school-age children, this AISD simplicity is genuinely valuable and often underweighted by buyers comparing to neighborhoods with attendance-zone variability. Verify with current AISD maps before pre-offer.
04
Pool is half the active set
42% of active Brentwood SFR have a pool (13 of 31), among the highest pool ownership rates in Central Austin. Pool premium is +17% on $/sqft. For families considering Brentwood with kids, the existing pool inventory is broader than you'll find in Hyde Park or Tarrytown, and at less of a premium than in Zilker.
05
Use the 111-day DOM as your clock
Median active DOM is 111 days, the slowest Central Austin market we track in 2026. There is zero "act fast" pressure. Take 7 to 10 days to inspect a new-build (new construction defect inspection is non-trivial), to verify lot size and tree-canopy retention, to model property tax assessment trajectory (new-build assessed value resets aggressively). Patience is fully rewarded.
06
Property tax check matters here
New-construction Brentwood homes assess high. A $1.7M new build commands ~$35K to $42K annual property tax (vs. ~$15K to $20K on the equivalent older cottage with seasoned homestead exemption). Always run a 5-year tax projection on any Brentwood new build before committing, the difference materially affects affordability and resale buyer pool. How a Brentwood Seller
Should Approach the 2026 Market

Brentwood sellers face a market where 61% of competing active inventory has reduced its price by an average of 9.3%. But every recent closing went at 100% of list. The market is binary: list precisely and sell, or list optimistically and reduce twice. Six tactical recommendations:

01
Price by tier, not by neighborhood median
"Brentwood median" combines original cottages and luxury new builds, useless as an anchor. 03
If you own new construction, address the spec saturation directly
21 active new builds competing on similar specs. Sellers who differentiate on a specific advantage (lot size, tree retention, ceiling height, indoor-outdoor flow, Brentwood Elementary walking) sell faster than sellers who default to generic "luxury new construction" copy. Identify the 2-3 specifics your home does better than the 20 competitors and lead with them.
04
Lead with the AISD-simplicity story

05
If you reduce, do it decisively at 30 days

A single 7 to 10% reduction at the 30-day mark, with refreshed photography and updated copy, restarts the listing as new inventory. Decisiveness beats incrementalism in this market.
06
If your lot is teardown-eligible, parallel-track it
Brentwood's teardown market is very active. For an original-condition cottage on a 7,500+ sqft lot, you have two buyer pools: regular owner-occupants and builder cash buyers. Sellers who run both processes simultaneously often extract 15 to 20% more from the builder pool than a conventional resale would yield, especially for properties near Burnet Road or Shipe Park.

The Walkability Premium

Walk Score 78 and
Brentwood's Price Advantage

Walk Score 78 is not just a marketing statistic for Brentwood, it is a quantifiable price premium that shows up in closed sales data. Walkability has become a documented buyer priority since 2020, and Brentwood's position as the most walkable neighborhood in North Central Austin translates directly into buyer demand. When comparable homes in Brentwood and Allandale sit on the market simultaneously, Brentwood homes consistently attract more showings and faster offers, all else being equal.

The mechanism is straightforward: every Brentwood home walks to Burnet Road. The walk time varies, a home on Brentwood Avenue (immediately east, one block from Burnet) might be a 3-minute walk to Epoch Coffee; a home on the western edge of the neighborhood might be 10 minutes. But every Brentwood home can reach the Burnet Road corridor without a car. That is not true for most of Allandale, most of Crestview, or most of Rosedale. Buyers who prioritize the ability to walk to dinner, particularly Domain tech workers who spend their mornings commuting north and want walkable evenings, price this access into their offers.

Luke Allen estimates the Brentwood walkability premium at 5 to 8% over equivalent Allandale or Crestview homes, representing $40,000 to $64,000 on an $800K baseline. This premium is not guaranteed on every transaction, but it is consistent enough that Luke Allen incorporates it into every Brentwood pricing analysis. Sellers who understand their walkability premium are less likely to underprice their listing against Allandale comps that don't apply. Buyers who understand it are less likely to offer below asking on a well-located Brentwood home and lose it to a better-informed buyer.

Location Factor

The Burnet Road Effect on
Brentwood Home Values

Burnet Road is Brentwood's eastern border, and unlike MoPac in Allandale, proximity to Burnet Road is generally an asset, not a liability. Burnet Road is a commercial corridor with one of Austin's most dense concentrations of restaurants, bars, coffee shops, and independent retail. The ability to walk to Epoch Coffee (the original Austin location), Foreign & Domestic (James Beard-recognized), Uchi (Tyson Cole's flagship), and Brentwood Social House from your home is a genuine lifestyle differentiator that Brentwood buyers pay for.

The Burnet Road proximity premium operates on a gradient. The blocks immediately adjacent to Burnet, the streets that back onto the Burnet corridor, are among the most walkable homes in Austin. These blocks command the highest per-square-foot prices within Brentwood. Moving west, the premium gradually diminishes as walk time to Burnet Road increases, though even the furthest western Brentwood blocks are only 10 to 12 minutes from Burnet Road. There is no Brentwood home that is more than a modest walk from the corridor.

The one nuance: homes on streets directly facing Burnet Road itself (as opposed to one block west) may experience slight traffic noise exposure, and buyers should evaluate this based on the specific block. Renovation Economics

Renovation vs. Sell As-Is
in Brentwood

The renovation decision in Brentwood is more nuanced than in Allandale or Crestview because Brentwood's walkability premium supports stronger as-is demand. Original-condition Brentwood homes sell relatively well compared to original-condition homes in less walkable North Central neighborhoods, buyers who prioritize walkability over finish level will accept an unupdated kitchen in exchange for a 5-minute walk to Epoch Coffee. This dynamic means the renovation-versus-as-is decision is genuinely less clear-cut in Brentwood than elsewhere.

The renovation economics look like this in Brentwood's current market: An original-condition 3/2 Brentwood home in the $650K to $750K range, with a well-executed kitchen, bath, and systems renovation costing $120K to $180K, can reasonably achieve a sale price of $950K to $1.2M. The gross renovation spread of $200K to $450K against a renovation cost of $120K to $180K produces a net return of $70K to $270K before carrying costs, taxes, and the risk of overruns. That is a meaningful return, but it is achieved only when the renovation is executed well, priced at fair market cost, and targeted at the updates Brentwood buyers actually value.

What generates strong ROI in Brentwood: kitchen updates that meet buyer expectations for finish quality, master bath renovations, fresh exterior paint, and landscaping that showcases the mature oak and pecan trees most Brentwood lots carry. What does not pencil: over-improvement that pushes price above what 78757 comps support, structural additions on modest-footprint homes, and high-end finishes that the buyer pool won't recognize as premium. Luke Allen runs the renovation analysis with real numbers for every Brentwood seller before any commitment is made. The honest answer depends entirely on the specific home, lot, and location within the neighborhood.

Renovation math at $700K baseline (Brentwood example): Original-condition home sells as-is for $700K. Full renovation cost: $140K. Expected post-renovation price: $1,000K to $1,050K. Net gain: $160K to $210K before carrying costs. ROI: positive, but not the 2021-era slam dunk. Teardown Market

The Brentwood
Teardown & Custom Build Market

The Brentwood teardown market is active and has been since approximately 2016. Custom builders and private buyers regularly purchase original-condition Brentwood ranches and cottages on viable lots, demolish the existing structure, and construct new custom homes that sell for $1.4M to $1.8M or higher. Brentwood's walkability premium, school clarity, and Burnet Road proximity make finished custom homes in 78757 competitive with new construction in any North Central Austin neighborhood.

Brentwood lots range from approximately 6,000 to 8,500 square feet, smaller than some Allandale lots but consistent in quality. Lots 6,500 sqft and above are the primary teardown targets, with land values currently ranging from $450K to $600K depending on location, dimensions, tree encumbrances, and proximity to Burnet Road or Shipe Park. The finished custom home economics work for builders when land is acquired at $500K to $550K, construction costs run $350 to $450 per sqft, and the finished product prices above $1.5M.

For Brentwood sellers with larger lots, understanding teardown value is essential before listing. A 7,200 sqft Brentwood lot carrying a 1,100 sqft original-condition cottage might be worth $520K as land, and that land value may be approaching or exceeding what a renovation of the same home would generate at resale.
