Where is the Austin tech corridor?

There isn't just one. Austin's tech jobs cluster in five corridors spread across the metro, from Taylor in the northeast to Oak Hill in the southwest:

  • Domain & Parmer Lane Apple, IBM, Amazon, Nvidia
  • I-35 & US-79 to Taylor Dell, Samsung, Blue Origin
  • SH-130, East & Southeast Tesla, Samsung Austin, Applied Materials
  • Downtown core Google, Meta, Indeed, Oracle
  • Southwest Parkway AMD, NXP

01

The Domain and Parmer Lane corridor

North and Northwest Austin, along MoPac, US-183 and Parmer Lane

Where it runs
From Burnet Road and the Domain north along MoPac and US-183 to Parmer Lane, then west toward Apple's campus and the 183A toll road into Cedar Park and Leander.
Who works here
Apple on West Parmer Lane, IBM on Burnet Road, Amazon and Nvidia around the Domain, and a deep bench of enterprise software and hardware offices in between.
Known for
Corporate campuses, enterprise software, hardware engineering.

How I would search here

Ask which side of Parmer Lane your badge reader is on before you choose a ZIP code. Apple pulls the search north and west, toward Avery Ranch, Cedar Park and Leander ISD. The Domain and IBM pull it the other way, toward Northwest Hills, Great Hills and Wells Branch. A home described as "near the Domain" can still mean crossing MoPac and US-183 twice a day, and that crossing is the part of this commute people most often underestimate.

Schools drive a lot of the price differences. The Leander ISD side of Cedar Park and the Round Rock ISD pockets around Avery Ranch tend to draw young families, while Northwest Hills trades newer construction for mature trees and a shorter drive into central Austin. 02

The I-35 and US-79 semiconductor corridor

Round Rock and Georgetown, then east through Hutto to Taylor

Where it runs
Up I-35 from North Austin through Pflugerville, Round Rock and Georgetown, then east on US-79 through Hutto to Taylor, with the SH-130 toll road running parallel on the east side.
Who works here
Dell Technologies at its world headquarters in Round Rock, Samsung at its chip fab campus in Taylor, and Blue Origin, which picked Hutto in October 2026 for its Constellation Park manufacturing campus on SH-79 (no opening date announced yet). Suppliers and contractors cluster around all three.
Known for
Chip fabrication, computer hardware, aerospace and large-scale manufacturing.

How I would search here

Most of what is for sale along this corridor is newer construction in master-planned communities. The median asking price across its ZIP codes is {{live:zip=78664,78665,78681,78626,78628,78633,78634,76574,78660:medianPrice}}, compared with {{live:city=Austin:medianPrice}} inside Austin city limits. That gap changes the questions worth asking. MUD and PID taxes, builder incentives and HOA rules move the monthly payment as much as the price does.

Direction matters too. A Dell product manager and a Samsung process engineer can both say "Round Rock" and want opposite ends of it: Dell sits right on I-35, while the Taylor fab is a straight run east on US-79. For Taylor and Blue Origin, Hutto and east Round Rock save the most time. For Dell, Georgetown and west Round Rock work well, and Pflugerville splits the difference for two-commute households.

03

The SH-130 corridor, East and Southeast Austin

SH-130 with US-290 East, SH-71 and northeast Parmer Lane

Where it runs
Down the SH-130 toll road on Austin's east side, where it crosses US-290 East toward Manor, SH-71 toward the airport and Bastrop, and Parmer Lane in northeast Austin.
Who works here
Tesla at Giga Texas, Samsung Austin Semiconductor in northeast Austin, Applied Materials at its Harris Branch campus on US-290 East, NXP at its Ed Bluestein site in East Austin, and SpaceX at its Starlink factory in Bastrop.
Known for
Electric vehicles, chip fabs, satellite and aerospace hardware, advanced manufacturing.

How I would search here

Shift schedules change the search. Many factory and fab roles run early or rotating shifts, so the drive at 5:30 a.m. matters more than the drive at rush hour, and a toll road you would skip on a weekday afternoon can be the right call at dawn. Easton Park and the Del Valle side put Giga Texas close. Manor and the 78754 side of northeast Austin do the same for Samsung Austin and Applied Materials.

Two things deserve a look on every east-side address before anyone writes an offer: where the airport flight paths run, and what is permitted on the empty land nearby, because industrial growth out here is still moving fast. Buyers who want a city neighborhood instead of a new subdivision usually land in East Austin and drive out against traffic.

The downtown tech core

The central business district, Lady Bird Lake and the Second Street district

Where it runs
The towers between Lady Bird Lake and the Capitol, Rainey Street, the Seaholm and Second Street district, and Oracle's campus on the south shore of the lake.
Who works here
Google on West 2nd Street, Meta, Indeed at Indeed Tower, Oracle at its Lakeshore campus, Silicon Labs at its headquarters, Salesforce, CrowdStrike, and most of the city's startups and venture firms.
Known for
Software, startups, venture capital, and the sales and go-to-market teams of big tech.

How I would search here

Downtown is the one corridor where you can skip the car commute entirely, and the trade is space for time. A condo within walking distance of the office has a median asking price of {{live:zip=78701;type=condo:medianPrice|well above the suburbs}}, before HOA dues, which vary a lot from tower to tower and belong in the budget from day one.

Buyers who want a yard usually look one ring out. Travis Heights is across the river from the towers, Hyde Park sits north near UT, and Mueller pairs newer homes with a short drive in. If Mueller is on your list, my Mueller to downtown commute breakdown covers it in detail.

05

The Southwest Parkway corridor

Southwest Austin and Oak Hill, the hub most lists leave out

Where it runs
Along Southwest Parkway between MoPac and SH-71, then through Oak Hill on US-290 West and William Cannon Drive, with West Lake Hills to the north and Dripping Springs to the west.
Who works here
AMD at its Southwest Parkway campus and NXP Semiconductors at its Oak Hill campus on William Cannon, plus the office parks around Lantana and Barton Creek.
Known for
Chip design and semiconductor manufacturing, set against the edge of the Hill Country.

How I would search here

Most summaries of Austin tech skip this corridor, which is a mistake for anyone working at AMD or NXP, because the homes closest to Southwest Parkway sit in some of the most sought-after school zones in the region. North of the parkway is Eanes ISD, where the median asking price is {{live:isd=Eanes:medianPrice|well into seven figures}}. South of it, Circle C and Oak Hill offer Austin ISD schools, greenbelt access and prices closer to the middle of the market.

Farther out, Dripping Springs trades a longer US-290 drive for acreage and a newer housing stock. For families weighing those school districts, my Eanes ISD home search lists every home for sale in the district.

Getting the map right

Four things the usual corridor lists get wrong

Search for Austin's tech corridor and you will see the same summary repeated across the web. It is close, but these four details change where you should be looking.

"Oracle is a north Austin employer"

Oracle sits on Lady Bird Lake

Its Austin campus is on the south shore of the lake, just east of downtown, which makes Travis Heights and East Austin the natural fits, not the Domain.

"Applied Materials is up at TechRidge"

Applied Materials' big campus is on US-290 East

Its main Austin site is the Harris Branch campus in northeast Austin, so Manor, Pflugerville and the 78754 area are its short commutes.

"Silicon Labs is a north-side company"

Silicon Labs is headquartered downtown

Its headquarters is in the downtown core, which puts it in the walk-to-work condo conversation alongside Google and Indeed.

"Austin has four tech hubs"

There is a fifth, in Southwest Austin

AMD's Southwest Parkway campus and NXP's Oak Hill site anchor a corridor most lists never mention, right next to Eanes ISD and Circle C.

Austin tech corridor FAQ

Short answers to what people ask before a move. For an employer-by-employer deep dive, the employer relocation playbooks go further, and the moving to Austin guide covers the rest of the move.

Where is the Austin tech corridor?

Austin does not have one tech corridor. It has five: the Domain and Parmer Lane area of North and Northwest Austin (Apple, IBM, Amazon, Nvidia); I-35 and US-79 running north through Round Rock to Hutto and Taylor (Dell, Samsung, Blue Origin); the SH-130 belt on the east and southeast side (Tesla, Samsung Austin Semiconductor, Applied Materials); downtown (Google, Meta, Indeed, Oracle); and Southwest Parkway in Southwest Austin (AMD, NXP).

What is Silicon Hills?

Silicon Hills is the nickname for the Austin area's tech economy, a nod to Silicon Valley and to the Texas Hill Country that starts on the west side of town. It does not name a single district. It covers all five corridors: the Domain and Parmer Lane, I-35 and US-79 up to Taylor, SH-130, downtown, and Southwest Parkway.

Where should I live if I work at Tesla Giga Texas?

Giga Texas sits at SH-130 and SH-71 on the southeast side, near the airport. The closest established neighborhoods are Easton Park and the Del Valle area (78617), where the median asking price is {{live:zip=78617:medianPrice|among the lowest inside the Austin area}}. People who want a city neighborhood often choose East Austin or Travis Heights and drive out on SH-71, and people who want land look toward Bastrop. Shift schedule matters as much as distance, so check the drive at the hour you would actually make it.

Is it cheaper to live near Samsung in Taylor?
