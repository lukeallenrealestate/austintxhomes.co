The Direct Answer

The best family-friendly Austin neighborhoods with good schools in 2026 are Tarrytown (Casis Elementary), Westlake / Eanes ISD, Barton Hills, Mueller, Hyde Park (Lee Elementary), Brentwood, Northwest Hills, and Zilker. Each combines strong AISD or Eanes ISD schools with walkable family amenities, parks, and safe streets. The Neighborhoods

The Eight Austin Neighborhoods Families Actually Choose

Austin has more than a dozen neighborhoods that families consider each year, but only a handful consistently deliver on both schools and daily livability. The eight below are where the majority of Austin families actually land after they finish comparing. Each has been ranked by school quality, neighborhood walkability, family amenities, and honest home-price reality.

Tarrytown (78703), Casis Elementary

Central Austin's prestige family neighborhood. Casis Elementary is one of AISD's highest-rated public elementary schools and covers about 78% of Tarrytown addresses. Feeds into O. Henry Middle and Austin High. Median active SFR list is $2,350,000, though the tier ranges from about $1M starter cottages to $5M+ estates. Streets are quiet, tree-canopied, and family-safe. Deep Eddy Pool and Reed Park are neighborhood anchors. Read the full Tarrytown market report for pricing detail.

Westlake / Eanes ISD (78746, 78733)

Eanes ISD is consistently ranked the top public school district in Texas. Elementary options include Bridge Point, Eanes, Cedar Creek, Forest Trail, Valley View, and Barton Creek Elementary. Feeds into Hill Country Middle and Westlake High School. Median home price is about $3,150,000 with lots averaging 0.629 acres, so families get real space in addition to the school. Buyers who prioritize schools above all else and can afford the price tier land here. See the Westlake / Eanes ISD market report.

Barton Hills (78704), Barton Hills Elementary

The family sweet spot for Central Austin. Barton Hills Elementary is highly rated within AISD and covers the whole neighborhood. Feeds into O. Henry Middle and Austin High (shared with Tarrytown). Location gives families direct greenbelt access, walkable proximity to Zilker Park and Barton Springs Pool, and downtown in under 10 minutes. Home prices sit meaningfully below Tarrytown but the family experience is comparable. Read the Barton Hills market report.

Hyde Park (78751), Lee Elementary

The most affordable AISD family neighborhood on this list. Lee Elementary is one of AISD's premium feeders, with a 29% per-square-foot premium visible in Hyde Park home prices ($576/sqft in the Lee zone vs. $448 in Ridgetop). Kealing Middle School gifted-and-talented program is the strongest AISD middle school option. Family stock is the Hyde Park bungalow, walkable to UT, coffee shops, and Shipe Park. Median SFR list is about $822,500. Read the Hyde Park market report.

Mueller (78723), Blazier + Kealing GT Access

The master-planned family neighborhood built on the old Mueller Airport site. Elementary is Blazier and Winn (with some Alliance Academy access), and the neighborhood association helps coordinate GT paths through Kealing. Mueller's appeal is the master-planned amenities: Lake Park, Mueller Lake dog park, Alamo Drafthouse, HEB, and the walkable town-center feel. Home prices range from about $500K condos to $1.5M+ single-family. Dell Medical proximity gives working parents unique daily convenience. See the Mueller market report.

Brentwood (78757), Brentwood Elementary

The North Central family neighborhood with the cleanest school feeder in Austin. Brentwood Elementary covers the whole neighborhood (no boundary split, unlike some AISD neighborhoods). Feeds into Lamar Middle and McCallum High. Median home price is about $1,200,000, but 42% of active inventory is sub-$1M, giving realistic entry points. Walk Score 78 and Shipe Park anchor daily family life. Read the Brentwood market report.

Zilker (78704), Zilker Elementary

The outdoor-lifestyle family choice. Zilker Elementary is highly rated within AISD. The neighborhood name comes from Zilker Park, Austin's premier family park (Barton Springs Pool, botanical garden, disc golf, playscape, Zilker train). Median SFR list is about $1,640,000 but 33% of active listings have pools and 42% of the sidewalk grid is walkable to the park. See the Zilker market report.

Northwest Hills (78731), Highland Park Elementary

The North-of-Camp-Mabry family option. Highland Park Elementary is well-rated within AISD, though attendance-zone specifics matter (some blocks feed Doss instead). Neighborhoods within Northwest Hills like Cat Mountain, Northwest Hills proper, and Balcones Park each have distinct family character. Home prices span from about $700K in older 1970s neighborhoods to $3M+ on prestige hillside streets. Popular with medical families due to St. David's proximity.

Frequently Asked

Austin Family Neighborhoods Questions Answered

What are the best family-friendly Austin neighborhoods with good schools?
The eight most consistently chosen by Austin families are: Tarrytown (Casis Elementary, AISD), Westlake / Eanes ISD, Barton Hills (Barton Hills Elementary, AISD), Hyde Park (Lee Elementary, AISD), Mueller, Brentwood (Brentwood Elementary, AISD), Zilker (Zilker Elementary, AISD), and Northwest Hills (Highland Park, AISD). Each combines strong schools with walkable amenities, parks, and family-safe streets.
Which Austin school district is the best?
Eanes ISD (Westlake area) is consistently ranked the top public school district in Texas. Within AISD, the strongest elementary feeders for the neighborhoods on this list are Casis (Tarrytown), Lee (Hyde Park), Barton Hills (Barton Hills), Brentwood (Brentwood and Crestview), Zilker (Zilker), and Highland Park (parts of Northwest Hills). Kealing Middle School gifted-and-talented is the strongest AISD middle school for academically driven students.
What is the most affordable family neighborhood in Austin with good schools?
Hyde Park (78751) with a median SFR list of about $822,500 offers the most affordable entry into a premier AISD feeder pattern (Lee Elementary, Kealing GT, McCallum High). Brentwood (78757) is the next tier at about $1,200,000 median but 42% of active listings are sub-$1M. Both are walkable, family-safe, and feed strong schools. See the Hyde Park report and Brentwood report.
Which Central Austin neighborhood has the best elementary school?
Casis Elementary in Tarrytown is consistently one of AISD's highest-rated elementary schools and covers about 78% of Tarrytown addresses. Barton Hills Elementary (Barton Hills), Lee Elementary (Hyde Park), and Zilker Elementary (Zilker) all rank very strongly within AISD. Every neighborhood has attendance-zone nuances, so verify the specific address at the AISD map before purchase.
Should we buy in Central Austin or move to a suburb for schools?
Depends on budget, commute tolerance, and lifestyle. Central Austin family neighborhoods (Tarrytown, Barton Hills, Hyde Park, Mueller, Brentwood) trade at higher prices but offer walkability, shorter commutes to major employers, and access to Austin's premier cultural amenities. Suburban options (Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Leander) offer lower prices, larger lots, and sometimes strong districts (Round Rock ISD, Leander ISD, Lake Travis ISD) at the cost of a real Austin commute. See the Central Austin vs. Round Rock comparison.
What is the safest Austin family neighborhood?
All eight neighborhoods on this list have safety records well above the Austin metro average and are considered family-safe by residents. Tarrytown, Westlake / Eanes ISD, Barton Hills, and Mueller have the strongest reputations. Safety within a neighborhood varies by block, and Luke walks every prospective family buyer through the specific street-level context of any address before showing.

What is the best Austin neighborhood for families with young kids?
Mueller is uniquely well-suited to families with young children due to its master-planned amenities: Lake Park, Mueller Lake dog park, walkable town center, and the Thinkery children's museum next door. Barton Hills, Brentwood, and Hyde Park also work very well for young families due to their walkable elementary schools, neighborhood parks, and quiet residential streets. Westlake and Tarrytown are more prestige-oriented but equally family-safe.
