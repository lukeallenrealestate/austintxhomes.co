Where AMD employees actually buy in Austin

Four submarkets, in the order I see them come up in my AMD client base:

  • Circle C (78749). The most common landing spot for AMD engineers. Family-friendly, master-planned, strong Austin ISD elementary schools, and a 15 to 20 minute commute to Southwest Parkway. If you want a suburban family neighborhood inside city limits with a short AMD commute, this is it.
  • Bee Cave (78738). Further west, more space for the money, top-rated Lake Travis ISD schools, upscale retail (Hill Country Galleria), and 20 to 25 minutes to AMD. This is where AMD families with a larger budget or a preference for newer construction end up.
  • Dripping Springs (78620). Even further out, Dripping Springs ISD, more acreage, more Hill Country feel. Some AMD principals and directors buy here for the property size and school quality despite the longer commute. Still typically under 30 minutes if you time the drive right.
  • Westlake / Eanes ISD (78746). The premium pick. Best public schools in the state, the shortest commute to AMD (15 minutes), and the highest home prices of the four. If your comp supports it, this is where the senior AMD employees I have worked with end up.

The Eanes ISD factor

If you can get into Eanes ISD, do it.

Eanes ISD (Westlake) has some of the highest-performing public schools in Texas. The demand for Eanes housing is structural, not cyclical, which means it protects both your kids' education AND your investment. My AMD clients with school-age kids consistently prioritize Eanes over any other single factor, including commute time, home age, or lot size.

The tradeoff is price. Westlake homes trade at a real premium to the rest of Southwest Austin. But the premium is durable, and if you ever need to sell, an Eanes address moves faster than almost anything else in the metro.

Eanes ISD is the one submarket where I tell AMD clients to stretch on price if the household budget can support it. The school district demand is structural and it protects your resale.

The commute reality

AMD employees consistently report a 15 to 25 minute commute from the four submarkets above to the Southwest Parkway campus:

  • Westlake: 15 minutes. Shortest commute.
  • Circle C: 15 to 20 minutes. William Cannon or Slaughter access, generally clean.
  • Bee Cave: 20 to 25 minutes. 71 into Oak Hill is the potential chokepoint but manageable outside peak.
  • Dripping Springs: 25 to 30 minutes. Bearable if you have flex time on start.

The AMD Southwest Parkway location is one of the better-situated Austin tech campuses for commute purposes. Unlike Tesla Gigafactory (east side) or Apple (north side), AMD sits directly next to the submarkets its engineers actually want to live in. That is a real quality-of-life advantage that does not show up in comp bands.

My take on rent vs buy for AMD hires

Always buy. Especially in Eanes ISD.

There will always be demand for a home in Eanes ISD, so you are not exposed if life at AMD changes and you need to move. The rest of Southwest Austin has a strong rental market broadly, so if you buy in Circle C or Bee Cave and need to relocate for any reason, renting your property out is straightforward. The downside of buying and then renting later is much smaller than the downside of paying 12 to 24 months of rent and watching your target neighborhood price out.

The AMD employees I have worked with who chose to rent first regularly regret it. The AMD employees who bought immediately have never told me they should have rented.

AMD RSUs and mortgage qualifying

AMD comp includes meaningful RSUs. This does not create mortgage-qualifying issues. Standard conventional loans work fine with AMD's comp structure. Some lenders are more comfortable underwriting RSU income than others, so I steer buyers to the lenders I know handle tech comp cleanly, but there is no structural problem with an AMD comp package.

Market timing right now

Austin is 1 to 2 months from the bottom of this cycle, in my read. Buyers who sat on the sidelines all summer are starting to move on the deals currently listed. Sellers who priced correctly are getting activity. Sellers who over-priced are dropping.

If you are relocating for AMD in Q4 2026 or Q1 2027, closing now beats waiting. Eanes ISD in particular sees demand recover before the broader market moves because school-district-driven demand is less rate-sensitive than typical Austin buyer demand.

Bottom line

Circle C, Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, and Westlake / Eanes ISD are the four submarkets my AMD clients actually land in. The commute to Southwest Parkway is 15 to 25 minutes from any of them. Eanes ISD is the highest-leverage single decision an AMD family with school-age kids can make. Always buy over rent. Do not wait for the obvious market bottom because by then Eanes has already moved.
