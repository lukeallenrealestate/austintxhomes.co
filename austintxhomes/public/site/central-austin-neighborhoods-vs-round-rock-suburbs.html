The Direct Answer

Central Austin neighborhoods vs. Round Rock suburbs in 2026 comes down to price vs. commute. Central Austin (Tarrytown, Barton Hills, Hyde Park, Mueller, Brentwood, Zilker) delivers 10 to 20 minute downtown commutes, walkable amenities, and AISD or Eanes ISD schools, at median SFR prices of $700K to $3M+. The Comparison

The Honest Central Austin vs. Round Rock Breakdown

The Central Austin vs. Round Rock decision is not about which area is better. It is about which set of tradeoffs matches your specific family, budget, commute reality, and lifestyle priorities. Here is the honest breakdown of every meaningful dimension.

Home Prices, Median Detached SFR

Central Austin family neighborhoods trade between $725K (Hyde Park) and $3M+ (Westlake / Eanes ISD, Tarrytown premium). Barton Hills, Mueller, Brentwood, Zilker sit in the $1M to $1.8M range. Round Rock median SFR is approximately $475K to $675K depending on subdivision. New construction in Round Rock at $500K to $625K typically delivers 2,200 to 3,000 square feet. Central Austin at $1.5M often delivers 1,800 to 2,400 square feet. The price-per-square-foot gap is significant.

Commute Time to Downtown Austin

Central Austin family neighborhoods sit 2.5 to 4.5 miles from downtown. Peak-hour drive times range from 10 to 25 minutes. Round Rock sits 18 to 22 miles north of downtown Austin. Peak-hour drive times range from 45 to 70 minutes northbound in evenings; 40 to 60 minutes southbound in mornings. For a downtown-employed worker, this is 60 to 90 minutes daily commute gap, or 250 to 375 hours per year. See the Mueller to downtown commute analysis for specific numbers.

Schools, AISD + Eanes ISD vs. Round Rock ISD

Central Austin schools include AISD premier feeders (Casis, Barton Hills, Lee, Brentwood, Zilker) and Eanes ISD (top-ranked district in Texas). Round Rock ISD is a strong district, well-regarded within Texas, with multiple highly-rated elementary and middle schools (Anderson Mill Elementary, Great Oaks Elementary, Cactus Ranch Elementary, Chandler Oaks Elementary). Neither is universally "better," but they have different characters: AISD delivers strong core-city feeders with racial and economic diversity; RRISD delivers newer facilities and more homogeneous suburban context.

Property Taxes and Total Cost of Ownership

Travis County city of Austin combined property tax rate in 2026 is approximately 2.1% to 2.3%. Round Rock (Williamson County + City of Round Rock + RRISD) combined rate is approximately 2.4% to 2.6%. On a $600K Round Rock home this is $14,400 to $15,600 annual property tax. On a $1.2M Central Austin home the tax is $25,200 to $27,600. The Central Austin home is more expensive in absolute dollars, but the effective rate is lower. Homestead exemption (must file by April 30 the year after purchase) applies in both areas and reduces the effective rate meaningfully.

Lifestyle and Amenities

Central Austin: walkable neighborhoods, Zilker Park and Barton Springs Pool, food halls, live music venues, hike-and-bike trail, farmers markets, deep restaurant scene. Round Rock: suburban master-planned amenities, Old Settlers Park, Round Rock Premium Outlets, IKEA, Kalahari Resort family water park (Round Rock), Dell Diamond baseball, quieter residential streets, larger backyards. Different lifestyles serving different priorities.

New Construction Availability

Round Rock has significantly more new-construction inventory. Central Austin new construction is rare and typically premium-priced. If you value new-construction warranties, modern floor plans, and turnkey systems, Round Rock delivers this at meaningfully lower prices. Central Austin buyers typically buy older homes with character and mature landscaping.

Resale and Appreciation

Central Austin family neighborhoods have historically appreciated faster than Round Rock over 10-year and 20-year windows due to constrained land supply and strong demand. Round Rock has appreciated meaningfully but at a slower pace. Both are strong resale markets in 2026. For long-term wealth building, Central Austin real estate has been the stronger vehicle historically; for immediate lifestyle affordability, Round Rock is often the better answer.

Who Chooses What

Central Austin is typically chosen by: tech workers commuting downtown, families prioritizing walkability, hybrid workers with 2-3 day downtown schedules, families with strong AISD or Eanes ISD preferences, buyers with $900K+ budgets. Round Rock is typically chosen by: families with $475K-$675K budgets, families prioritizing new construction, remote workers or workers commuting to North Austin / IBM Burnet / Dell campus, families prioritizing larger lots and quieter streets.

Central Austin vs. Round Rock Questions Answered

What is the median home price in Central Austin vs. Round Rock?
Central Austin family neighborhoods have median detached SFR prices ranging from about $725K in Hyde Park to $3M+ in Westlake / Eanes ISD, with Barton Hills, Mueller, Brentwood, and Zilker in the $1M to $1.8M range. Round Rock median SFR is approximately $475K to $675K depending on subdivision. New-construction Round Rock at $500K to $625K typically delivers 2,200 to 3,000 square feet vs. 1,800 to 2,400 square feet at Central Austin premium price tiers.
How much longer is the commute from Round Rock to downtown Austin?
Round Rock sits 18 to 22 miles north of downtown Austin. Peak-hour drive times to downtown are 45 to 70 minutes northbound in evenings and 40 to 60 minutes southbound in mornings. Central Austin family neighborhoods (Mueller, Barton Hills, Hyde Park, Tarrytown) sit 2.5 to 4.5 miles from downtown with peak-hour drive times of 10 to 25 minutes. Daily commute gap is 60 to 90 minutes, or 250 to 375 hours per year for a downtown-employed worker.
Are Round Rock ISD schools as good as AISD or Eanes ISD?
Round Rock ISD is a strong district, well-regarded within Texas, with multiple highly-rated elementary and middle schools. Eanes ISD (Westlake area) is consistently ranked the top public school district in Texas. AISD includes elite feeders (Casis, Barton Hills, Lee, Brentwood, Zilker) and less-rated schools depending on address. Neither RRISD nor AISD is universally "better", but they have different characters: AISD delivers strong core-city feeders with more diversity; RRISD delivers newer facilities and more homogeneous suburban context.
How do property taxes compare?
Travis County city of Austin combined property tax rate in 2026 is approximately 2.1% to 2.3%. Round Rock (Williamson County + City of Round Rock + RRISD) combined rate is approximately 2.4% to 2.6%. Effective rate is slightly higher in Round Rock, though absolute dollar tax on a $600K Round Rock home ($14,400 to $15,600) is significantly lower than on a $1.2M Central Austin home ($25,200 to $27,600). Homestead exemption applies in both areas and reduces effective rate meaningfully. File by April 30 the year after purchase.
Which is better for families with young kids?
Depends on priorities. Central Austin family neighborhoods (Mueller, Barton Hills, Hyde Park, Zilker, Tarrytown) offer walkable elementary schools, neighborhood parks (Zilker, Mueller Lake, Deep Eddy), and a vibrant family-culture feel with easy access to museums (Thinkery, Blanton, Bullock). Round Rock offers larger lots, quieter streets, master-planned amenities (Old Settlers Park, Kalahari water park), and generally more space per dollar. Both work well for young families; the choice comes down to whether walkability or space matters more.
Which is better for tech workers commuting downtown?
Central Austin is significantly better for tech workers with downtown or near-downtown offices (Google Sail Building, Meta Colorado Tower, Indeed HQ, Oracle waterfront). Round Rock adds 60 to 90 minutes of daily commute time, which for a hybrid tech worker with 3 days a week downtown is 180 to 270 minutes weekly (3 to 4.5 hours). For fully remote workers or North Austin workers (IBM Burnet, Dell Round Rock), Round Rock is often the better choice due to price and space.
Which appreciates faster?
Central Austin family neighborhoods have historically appreciated faster than Round Rock over 10-year and 20-year windows due to constrained land supply and strong demand. Round Rock has appreciated meaningfully but at a slower pace. Both are strong resale markets in 2026. For long-term wealth building, Central Austin real estate has been the stronger historical vehicle; for immediate lifestyle affordability, Round Rock is often the better answer.
What about new construction?
Round Rock has significantly more new-construction inventory. Central Austin new construction is rare and typically premium-priced (custom or luxury tier). If you value new-construction warranties, modern floor plans, energy-efficient systems, and turnkey ownership, Round Rock delivers this at meaningfully lower prices. Central Austin buyers typically buy older homes with character and mature landscaping.
