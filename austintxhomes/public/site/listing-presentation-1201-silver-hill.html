Execution strategy

Terminate, refresh, relist.

Terminate the current contract

Quick call with the title company to confirm position. If still in option period, termination is clean. If past option, buyer release may require nominal consideration (typical range $500 to $5,000).

Refresh the marketing package

New MLS entry resets the public days-on-market clock. Zillow and Redfin will show the historical price. Mitigate with fresh photography, drone shots, twilight exteriors, and staging. Budget $1,500 to $2,500.

Pre-market exclusive preview

7 to 14 day "coming soon" window before activating the MLS. Exclusive showings to pre-qualified Westlake luxury buyer agents. Builds urgency and generates pre-launch interest.

Launch at $2,695,000

Target marketing window: 30 to 60 days at the recommended list price. First 7-10 days drive the strongest interest; use that window for showings and set an offer-review deadline if demand warrants.

Negotiate to $2,625,000 to $2,650,000

Buyers in this price band expect some negotiation room. The list price is calibrated to land the sale in that window. Net result: $630,000 to $655,000 more than the current contract.
