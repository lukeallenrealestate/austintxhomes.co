Execution strategy

Terminate, refresh, relist.

Terminate the contract

Quick call to the title company to confirm your position. Clean if still in option period. If past option, buyer release might run $500-$5,000.

Refresh the marketing

A new MLS entry resets the DOM clock, but Zillow and Redfin keep the price history. Counter that with new photography, drone, twilight exteriors, and light staging. Budget $1,500-$2,500.

Coming-soon preview, 7-14 days

Invite-only showings for Westlake luxury buyer agents before the MLS goes live. Builds a queue of serious buyers for day one.

Launch at $2,245,000

Close enough to the proven-clearing level that we'll see showing activity in the first week. If more than two serious buyers surface, set an offer deadline.

Land the sale at $2,150,000-$2,200,000

Buyers at this tier expect a little room. The list is calibrated to land here. Net gain over the current contract: $155,000-$205,000.
