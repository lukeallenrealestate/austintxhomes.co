1. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

When you submit a form, request a consultation, ask for a property valuation, schedule a showing, save a search, or contact us in any other way, you may provide:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property address (yours or one you're interested in)
  • Buying or selling timeline, budget, and goals
  • Specific property preferences (school district, neighborhood, price range, square footage, lot size)
  • Any message or notes you choose to include
  • For relocating buyers: current state, employer, and timing of move (when you tell us)

Information collected automatically

When you visit austintxhomes.co, we and certain third parties (see below) automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location (city/region only)
  • Browser type, device type, operating system, and screen size
  • Pages visited, links clicked, and time spent on the site
  • Referring website (e.g. whether you came from Google, Facebook, or a direct link)
  • Search terms you entered in our property search tool

Information from third parties

If you message us through Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or another platform, we may receive your name, contact info, and profile data from that platform. If a lender or relocation specialist refers you to us with your permission, we may receive your contact info from them.

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries about buying, selling, or relocating
  • Send you property listings, market updates, and neighborhood information that match what you're looking for
  • Schedule showings, consultations, and meetings
  • Provide guides, market reports, and the cash-flow property alerts you opted into
  • Comply with our legal and professional obligations as a licensed Texas Realtor under TREC
  • Operate and improve the website, including monitoring performance, fixing bugs, and improving search results
  • Measure the effectiveness of our marketing (including Facebook and Google ads) so we can spend less money showing ads to people who aren't interested
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or unauthorized access

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent it. We do not trade it.

3. When we share information

We share information only when necessary, and only with parties bound by their own privacy obligations. Specifically:

  • Service providers who help us run the business, including our email host (Gmail / Google Workspace), our CRM, our hosting provider, our analytics tools, and our cloud storage (Cloudflare R2). These vendors only get the data they need to do their job.
  • Cooperating real estate professionals when a transaction requires it, for example, the listing agent on a home you want to tour, or a buyer's agent representing the other side of a deal.
  • Title companies, lenders, inspectors, and other transaction parties when you've engaged us to represent you in a purchase or sale and these parties need your information to do their job.
  • Advertising platforms (Meta, Google), but only in aggregated or hashed form for ad targeting and measurement. See sections 4 and 5 below.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by subpoena, court order, or other valid legal process, or to investigate suspected fraud.
  • A successor if Austin Marketing + Development Group is acquired, merges, or transfers assets, in which case your information would transfer to the successor under this same policy.

4. Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to make the site work, remember your preferences, and measure performance. Specifically:

  • Essential cookies, needed for the site to function (e.g. session cookies, form anti-spam tokens). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies, help us understand which pages perform well and where users get stuck. The data is aggregated and not used to identify you individually.
  • Advertising cookies, set by Meta Pixel (see next section). These let us show relevant ads to people who have visited our site, and measure whether those ads led to inquiries.

You can control cookies through your browser's settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies entirely. Some site features may not work correctly if you do.

5. Meta (Facebook) Pixel

We use the Meta Pixel, a small piece of tracking code from Meta Platforms, Inc., to measure our Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns. The Pixel may collect:

  • The page URL you visited on austintxhomes.co
  • Standard browser metadata (browser, device, IP)
  • Whether you completed certain actions, like submitting a contact form (we send Meta a "Lead" event without your name, email, or phone, only the fact that a lead occurred)
  • A Meta-set browser cookie that may identify you on Facebook or Instagram if you're logged in

Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy and may use it to show you targeted ads. You can manage how Meta uses this data, including opting out of tracking, through your Facebook ad preferences at facebook.com/adpreferences, or through your browser's tracking-protection settings.

6. MLS & IDX listing data

Property listings shown on austintxhomes.co are sourced from the Austin/Central Texas Realty Information Service (ACTRIS) MLS through an authorized IDX feed (MLS GRID). Listing data, addresses, prices, photos, descriptions, is provided by the listing brokerage and is the property of ACTRIS or the respective listing broker.

If you save a search or favorite a property, we store that selection so we can show it to you again and send you alerts when matching listings come on the market. We do not share your saved-search activity with anyone outside our operation.

7. Calls, text messages & TCPA consent

By providing your phone number through one of our forms, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Luke Allen and Austin Marketing + Development Group about real estate services you've inquired about. Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message, or by asking us to remove you from our call list. Opting out of marketing messages does not opt you out of transactional messages tied to an active real estate transaction (e.g. closing-day coordination).

8. How long we keep your information

We keep your personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes described in this policy, plus any time required by law. Specifically:

  • Active client files, kept for the duration of the relationship plus four (4) years after the last transaction, per TREC record-retention requirements.
  • Inquiry-only contact records (form submissions where no transaction occurred), kept up to three (3) years from your last interaction, then deleted.
  • Email subscribers (market reports, cash-flow alerts), kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Website analytics and ad measurement data, kept up to 26 months in aggregated form.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner, see "Your rights" below.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including:

  • HTTPS encryption across the entire website
  • Encrypted-at-rest storage for our databases and file uploads
  • Limited access, only Luke Allen and authorized administrators can access contact records
  • Two-factor authentication on all administrative accounts
  • Regular review of our hosting providers' security posture

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable Texas and federal law.

10. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can ask us to:

  • See what personal information we have about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Delete your information (subject to TREC record-retention requirements for active or completed transactions)
  • Stop emailing or texting you for marketing purposes

To exercise any of these rights, email Luke@austinmdg.com with your request. We aim to respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

11. California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you're a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act:

  • The right to know what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and share
  • The right to delete personal information we hold about you
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information
  • The right to opt out of "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, which in our case relates to the Meta Pixel
  • The right to limit use and disclosure of sensitive personal information
  • The right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights

We do not sell personal information for money. We do disclose limited browsing activity to Meta for ad targeting, which California law treats as "sharing." To opt out of this sharing, email Luke@austinmdg.com with the subject line "California Opt-Out" and we will exclude your browser from our Meta audience-building.

12. Texas residents (TDPSA)

Effective July 1, 2024, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives Texas residents rights similar to California's. You may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of your personal data, and may opt out of targeted advertising. To exercise these rights, email Luke@austinmdg.com.

You may also appeal a denied request by replying to our response. If you believe your appeal was not handled properly, you can contact the Texas Attorney General at texasattorneygeneral.gov.

13. Children's privacy

austintxhomes.co is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Third-party links

Our site may link to third-party websites, official sources like the Texas Real Estate Commission, the IRS, school district websites, or news articles. We don't control these sites and aren't responsible for their privacy practices. Check each site's policy before sharing information with them.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our practices change, or as new laws take effect. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we'll provide more prominent notice (such as a banner on the homepage or an email to subscribers).
