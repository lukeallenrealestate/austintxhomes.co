The Sub-Market Breakdown

Where Modern Pool Homes Actually Cluster in 78704

The 78704 zip code is Austin's premium inner-loop corridor. Every modern pool home listed here trades in the top tier of Austin residential real estate. Here is the honest breakdown of where these homes actually are, what "modern" means in each sub-market, and the specific price and pool-quality context.

Bouldin Creek: The New-Construction Modern Core

Bouldin Creek has the highest concentration of true new-construction modern pool homes in 78704. Custom spec builders (LGI, Rockit, StudioMET Architects) have delivered 40+ modern SFR builds in the last three years, most with plunge pools or lap pools tucked into 5,000 to 8,000 sqft lots. Typical modern spec: 2,800 to 4,200 sqft, 3 to 5 bed, floor-to-ceiling glass, indoor-outdoor plan, engineered pool. Price: $1.8M to $3.5M. See the family neighborhoods guide if schools also matter.

Zilker: Renovated Moderns and Custom Rebuilds

Zilker has fewer ground-up new builds than Bouldin Creek but a strong pipeline of down-to-studs modernizations of 1960s to 1980s ranches. Zilker Park proximity and Zilker Elementary AISD feeder make this the modern-family sweet spot. Pool homes in Zilker typically run $1.5M to $2.8M. Deep renovation quality varies significantly; verify structural work quality and permit compliance before offer. See the Zilker market report for pricing detail.

Travis Heights: Mid-Century Modern Pool Renovations

Travis Heights (east of Congress) has the largest inventory of mid-century modern homes with pools, many from the original 1950s to 1970s architectural wave. Renovated MCM pool homes here trade between $1.2M and $2.5M, with occasional under-renovated tear-down candidates at $900K to $1.1M. Travis Heights lot sizes are larger than Bouldin (7,000 to 12,000 sqft) which supports better pool designs. Walkable to South Congress restaurants and Lady Bird Lake trail.

Barton Hills: Contemporary Renovations Near the Greenbelt

Barton Hills homes with pools are often 1970s to 1990s builds that have been contemporary-remodeled. Barton Hills Elementary AISD feeder is a major draw. Pool homes here run $1.4M to $2.6M. Barton Creek Greenbelt access from Barton Hills is a genuine outdoor-lifestyle differentiator. See the Barton Hills market report.

South Lamar Corridor: Newer Builds Between Zilker and Barton Hills

The South Lamar Blvd corridor between Zilker and Barton Hills is seeing infill modern construction with pools on the smaller lots (5,000 to 6,500 sqft) that dominate the corridor. Modern pool homes here trade $1.4M to $2.4M. Walk-in restaurant scene (South Lamar) and Zilker Park proximity are the primary draws.

Pool Premium and Ongoing Cost

A pool adds approximately 8% to 14% to a 78704 home's market value vs. a comparable non-pool listing. Ongoing cost: $300 to $500 per month for maintenance, chemicals, and equipment reserves. Pool insurance surcharge on homeowners policy: $150 to $250 per year. Older pools (pre-2000) may need $15K to $40K in restorations (replaster, coping, equipment). Every buyer should get a pool inspection separate from the home inspection.

What "Modern" Actually Means in 78704 Listings

"Modern" in the 78704 listing world means different things. True modern architecture (Bouldin new-construction, StudioMET builds): flat or low-slope roof, floor-to-ceiling glass, exposed steel or wood beams, minimal ornament, open kitchen. Mid-century modern (Travis Heights renovations): 1950s to 1970s original bones with contemporary finish updates. Contemporary renovation (Barton Hills, Zilker): 1970s to 1990s ranches or split-levels with modernized kitchens, baths, and open plans but original architectural envelope. Verify which type you're looking at before comparing prices.

What to Verify Before Offer

For every 78704 modern pool home, verify: (1) pool age, equipment condition, and permit history, (2) HOA status (many 78704 pockets are HOA-free, some new-construction subdivisions have HOAs), (3) drainage and flood plain proximity (parts of 78704 near Barton Creek and Bouldin Creek have flood exposure), (4) noise exposure (South Lamar, South Congress, Barton Springs Rd are loud), (5) tree canopy (heritage trees affect what you can build), and (6) school attendance zone at the specific address (Zilker vs. Barton Hills vs. Becker feeder matters).
