Background

Built for Austin

Austin is one of the most dynamic real estate markets in the country, and one of the most misunderstood. The difference between getting a great deal and overpaying, or between a home that fits your life and one that looks good on paper, comes down to local knowledge. Not just knowing which zip codes are popular, but understanding each street, each neighborhood's trajectory, and what a property is actually worth today.

I built my career on that depth of knowledge. I know which Westlake streets have the best Hill Country views and which ones back up to a highway. I know which Mueller blocks are walkable to the best coffee and which South Congress listings are priced on hope. That granular, on-the-ground knowledge is what I bring to every transaction.

Questions About Luke Allen

Will I work with Luke directly, or with a team?

Directly. Luke does not run a team, so the person who takes your first call is the same person who tours with you, writes and negotiates your offer, and walks you through the inspection, appraisal, and closing table. Nobody junior picks up your file halfway through.

Where can I read Luke Allen's client reviews?

On his Google Business Profile, where 41 clients have left reviews and every one of them is five stars. The reviews are posted under clients' own names and cover first-time purchases, luxury sales, and relocations.

How do I confirm Luke Allen holds a Texas real estate license?

Go to the Texas Real Estate Commission's public license holder lookup and enter license number 788149 or the name Luke Allen. The record shows license status and the sponsoring broker, Austin Marketing + Development Group. Any Texas agent you are thinking of hiring should pass the same lookup.

What kinds of clients does Luke Allen represent?

Buyers at every stage, from first-time purchases to luxury homes, plus sellers, rental property investors, families relocating from out of state, and buyers shopping new construction who want someone on their side of the builder's contract.

Does Luke Allen work outside central Austin?

Yes. Most of his work is in central and west Austin neighborhoods such as Tarrytown, Clarksville, Zilker, Hyde Park, and Westlake Hills, but he also represents clients in Round Rock, Cedar Park, Rollingwood, and the newer master-planned communities around the metro. If you are unsure whether your area is covered, just ask.
