Why this matters

The Zestimate is not your home's value.

Texas is a non-disclosure state. Closed residential sale prices in Texas are not public record. The MLS feeds them only to licensed members. That means every automated home-value tool, Zestimate, Redfin Estimate, Realtor.com estimate, is built on inferred data from tax assessments and public listings only. On Austin homes they commonly miss by 10 to 25 percent either direction, and the miss gets wider above $800,000 and below $350,000 where the comparable sample thins out. A Realtor-prepared CMA uses the actual closed prices.
