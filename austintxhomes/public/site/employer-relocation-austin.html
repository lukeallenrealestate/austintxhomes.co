Common cross-cutting themes

Three themes that run through every tech-employer Austin move

Regardless of employer, three structural themes shape most Austin tech relocation decisions. Each playbook addresses the employer-specific version of these themes.

Theme 1

Equity compounds base salary

RSU appreciation (especially at Nvidia, Meta, Apple, Amazon through the 2023-2026 window) materially expands realistic buying power beyond base-salary math. Many mid-career ICs land one submarket tier higher than base-pay bands would predict.

Theme 2

Bay Area equity transfer

Inbound Austin transferees from California tech cities often arrive with $1-3M of Bay Area home equity rolling into the Austin purchase. Combined with Texas's 60-75% housing cost reduction for comparable property, this routinely enables all-cash or 50%+ down transactions.

Theme 3

Texas tax arbitrage

The absence of state income tax (vs California 13.3%, New York 10.9%) compounds across multi-year compensation into material long-term wealth retention. For employees relocating from CA, NY, or IL, the tax savings often justify the move independently of other factors.
