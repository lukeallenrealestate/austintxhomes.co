The 78701 Downtown Playbook

What Actually Moves the Needle When Selling a 78701 Condo

Downtown Austin condo pricing is a building-by-building, floor-by-floor problem. The Austonian floor-15 and the Austonian penthouse are not the same market. The W and 44 East Ave are not the same product. Get the building comps right and everything else follows.

Building-Specific Comps Are the Whole Game

Every 78701 tower trades on its own comp sheet. The Austonian, W Residences, 44 East Ave, The Independent, Northshore, Milago, Seaholm Residences, Skyhouse, and the Sabine each have distinct pricing dynamics. Pricing your unit off a generic "downtown Austin condo" average produces a wrong answer 100% of the time. Luke pulls building-specific closed comps for every listing.

Floor Level Premium Math

Downtown Austin buildings typically carry a 3% to 6% floor premium per 10 floors of elevation, compounding at the top. A Floor 5 unit at the Austonian and an otherwise identical Floor 45 unit can trade at a 25%+ price differential. Your listing needs to price to your specific floor, not the building median.

View Premium (Waterfront, City, Capitol)

Lady Bird Lake water views command 15% to 25% premiums over unobstructed city views, which command 10% to 15% premiums over interior-block views. Capitol views carry a smaller but distinct premium. Your listing photography and copy need to make the view legible; buyers cannot price what they cannot see.

The HOA Fee Structure Impacts Sale Price

Downtown Austin HOA fees range from $500/month at older stock to $2,000+/month at full-amenity towers. Buyers price total monthly cost, not just mortgage. A $700K unit with a $1,800 HOA competes with an $800K unit at a $700 HOA on effective monthly. Your listing has to frame the HOA value proposition explicitly.

Downtown Employer Walk-Score

The single biggest 78701 buyer profile in 2026 is the tech employee at Google (Sail Building), Meta (Colorado Tower), Indeed (200 W 6th), or Oracle (waterfront). Sub-5-minute walking distance to their office is a real feature buyers pay for. Your listing needs to name the walk time to specific downtown employers, not just "walkable."

Investor vs. Owner-Occupant Buyer Pool

The 78701 buyer pool splits into two: full-time owner-occupants who value amenities and views, and investors (STR-permitted units are rare; long-term rental cap-rate driven). Owner-occupants consistently pay 8% to 15% more per square foot than investors. Your marketing plan should optimize for the owner-occupant pool unless your unit is specifically STR-eligible.

2BR + Den > 1BR at Every Tier

The strongest 78701 resale profile is 2BR + den or 3BR at 1,200-1,800 sqft. 1BR studios trade slower and reduce more often. If your unit is a 1BR, price it aggressively on day one; a 60-day sit becomes a 5% to 10% reduction that never recovers.

Parking Is a Line Item, Not a Detail

Assigned parking spaces trade for $30K to $75K depending on building and location within the garage. If your unit has 2 spaces, that is real value; if it has 0 spaces (rare), your comps must reflect that. Do not assume parking is priced into the listing; make it explicit.

Frequently Asked

78701 Sellers Questions Answered

How much does floor level actually affect price in a downtown Austin condo?
Typically 3% to 6% per 10 floors, compounding at the top. A Floor 5 unit and a Floor 45 unit in the same building can trade at a 20% to 30% price differential purely on elevation and view.
What is the average HOA fee for a 78701 condo?
HOA fees range from $500 to $700/month at older buildings (Milago, some parts of Northshore) to $1,500 to $2,200/month at full-amenity towers (Austonian, W Residences, The Independent). Buyers price total monthly cost, so the HOA line is critical.
Which downtown Austin building sells fastest?
Historically, well-priced 2BR units at the Austonian, The Independent, and 44 East Ave move in under 30 days. 1BR studios at any building move slower. Full-amenity buildings with concierge sell 25% faster than limited-amenity buildings at the same price point on average.
Can I short-term rent my 78701 condo?
Most 78701 buildings have HOA rules that restrict or prohibit short-term rentals (under 30 days). A few buildings allow STR with permits. Do not assume STR eligibility; check your HOA docs specifically before marketing STR income potential to buyers.
How do I price my parking space?
Assigned deeded parking spaces trade for $30,000 to $75,000 in 78701 depending on building, level, and proximity to unit. If your unit includes 2 spaces, add that value line to your listing explicitly. Guest-only or unassigned parking has no add-back value.
What is the biggest mistake 78701 sellers make?
Pricing to the building median instead of to their specific unit's floor, view, and finish level. The Austonian ranges from $650K to $6M+; picking the middle of that range is not a strategy.
Should I stage a downtown condo?
Yes, especially for units under $900K where the buyer pool is broader and expects showroom-condition visualization. Above $1.5M, buyers can typically imagine the space; light staging plus professional cleaning is usually enough.
Do downtown condos appreciate faster than SFR?
Historically no; 78701 condo appreciation has trailed detached Central Austin SFR on a 10-year basis, though the top-tier buildings (Austonian, W, Independent) have kept pace. Building selection matters enormously.
