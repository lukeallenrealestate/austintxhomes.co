Band 1 · Under $300K

Entry tier: under $300K

Outer-metro and rural-edge communities. 35-45 min to central Austin.

Below $300K the viable Austin-area options are outer-metro and rural-edge communities east of Austin or far south. The City of Austin core is largely out of reach at this band except for occasional condo or townhome inventory. Longer commute in exchange for the lowest acquisition cost in the Austin metro.

Inner-ring: $400K-$500K

Austin ISD + Pflugerville ISD + Leander ISD inventory opens up. 20-30 min commute.

Band 4 · $500K+

Approaching the FHA loan limit: $500K+

Central Austin character opens up. FHA loan limit around $524K for Travis County.

Above $500K the first-time buyer landscape widens significantly. Central-Austin character neighborhoods become reachable, including Mueller, select East Austin pockets, and older established South Austin. The right band for any specific buyer depends on buying power (base income plus FTHB program stack), commute requirements, school district priorities if applicable, and lifestyle preferences between suburban and central-Austin character.

How to pick the right band for your Austin FTHB purchase

The practical selection process runs through three questions. First: what is your realistic combined buying power including FTHB program stack? Base loan plus TSAHC or TDHCA DPA plus any City of Austin DPA plus gift funds plus savings down payment equals the total. Many Austin FTHBs end up with more buying power than they initially estimate because they underestimate the program stack.

Second: what is your commute requirement? Working downtown constrains the shortlist to inner-ring suburbs plus central Austin. Working at Dell Round Rock, Apple Parmer, Amazon Tech Hub Domain, or Tesla Gigafactory each pulls toward different band options. Fully remote work opens up the outer-metro bands at lower cost.

Third: do you have school-age children with specific district priorities? Leander ISD and Round Rock ISD typically require Band 3 or Band 4 budget. Pflugerville ISD and Hays ISD work in Band 3. Rural ISDs (Elgin, Manor, Hutto) work in Band 1 and Band 2.

FTHB neighborhoods FAQ

Common FTHB neighborhood questions

What are the best Austin neighborhoods for first-time buyers under $300K?

Outer-metro and rural-edge communities. Elgin 78621 (362 active @ $309K, 35-45 min commute), Manor 78653 (292 active @ $342K, 25-35 min), lower end of Kyle 78640 (433 active @ $357K, 25-35 min). Core City of Austin largely out of reach under $300K except occasional condo/townhome.

What Austin neighborhoods work for first-time buyers in the $300K-$400K band?

Sweet spot of Austin suburban FTHB market. Hutto 78634 (479 @ $365K), Kyle 78640 (433 @ $357K), Georgetown 78626 (279 @ $368K), Round Rock 78664 (188 @ $364K), San Marcos 78666 (264 @ $366K), upper Manor 78653. Distinct ISDs, commutes, HOA character.

What Austin neighborhoods work for first-time buyers in the $400K-$500K band?

Inner-ring with Austin-metro ISDs and better commutes. Pflugerville 78660 (426 @ $415K), Buda 78610 (340 @ $412K), Cedar Park 78613 under $500K (146 @ $427K Leander ISD Westwood High feeder), Round Rock 78681 lower (110 @ $489K), East Austin 78724 (79 @ $392K).

What Austin neighborhoods are available for first-time buyers above $500K?

Central-Austin character opens up. Mueller 78723 condo/townhome lower tier $500K-$600K, East Austin 78721 and 78722 (140 @ $522K), Round Rock 78681 full tier, South Austin 78745 older established, upper Pflugerville 78660, Southeast Austin 78744 and 78747 Easton Park corridor. FHA loan limit (~$524K Travis County) is the practical ceiling for pure FHA.
