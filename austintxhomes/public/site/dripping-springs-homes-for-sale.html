What school district serves Dripping Springs?
Dripping Springs ISD - consistently one of the top public school districts in Texas. Elementary feeders include Walnut Springs, Rooster Springs, Sycamore Springs, and Dripping Springs Elementary. Middle schools are Sycamore Springs Middle and Dripping Springs Middle. The high school is Dripping Springs High. Luke Allen verifies feeder assignment for every address.
