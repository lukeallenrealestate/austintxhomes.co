The historically STR-friendly Austin-area submarkets

These submarkets tend to run permissive regulatory environments for non-owner-occupied STR with established vacation-rental operating infrastructure (cleaners, property managers, insurance providers familiar with STR). Still verify specific property HOA and current regulations before closing.

Dripping Springs

78620 · Hays County ETJ

Hill Country wedding and wine-trail STR demand. Historically friendlier regulatory environment than City of Austin. Larger lots support bachelorette and multi-family-group formats.

Wimberley

78676 · Hays County

Established vacation-rental tourism market anchored by Blue Hole, Jacob's Well, river tubing. Mature operating infrastructure and recurring guest demand.

Spicewood

78669 · Burnet / Travis

Lake Travis west shore vacation STR. Water-access premium plus Hill Country character. HOA varies by subdivision; Barton Creek Lakeside restrictive, other areas friendlier.

Horseshoe Bay

78657 · Burnet County

Lake LBJ resort STR corridor with established vacation-rental infrastructure, Horseshoe Bay Resort proximity. Golf and lake-access demand.

Fredericksburg

78624 · Gillespie County

Texas Hill Country wine country, strong established STR market, high peak-season nightly rates. 90 minute drive from Austin, often cross-marketed.

The verify-before-you-offer Austin submarkets

These submarkets vary block to block and property to property. Some addresses work for STR, others do not. Pre-purchase verification of HOA, zoning, and city license eligibility is essential before contract.

Downtown Austin condos

78701

City code may permit, but building HOAs frequently restrict independent of city code. Confirm building-specific STR rules before offering. The Independent, Fifth and West, 44 East, Rainey towers each have distinct policies.

East Austin

78702, 78721, 78722

Older single-family in historically zoned areas often works. Newer subdivisions sometimes have HOA restrictions. Block-level verification required. Culture-food-adjacent STR demand remains strong.

South Austin

78704, 78745

Established neighborhoods frequently permit. Newer condo buildings and master-planned subdivisions vary significantly. SoCo and Zilker character neighborhoods often work; verify specific property.

Zilker + Travis Heights

78704 pockets

High STR demand due to downtown proximity and SXSW / ACL event-weekend premium. Older bungalow housing stock often permits but newer-build teardowns may have different zoning overlay. Verify specifically.

Lake Travis non-master-planned

78734, 78669 (non-HOA areas)

Lake Travis vacation STR works in non-HOA-restricted waterfront areas but master-planned communities within the same zip codes typically restrict. Verify by subdivision.

Older established central

78703, 78723 (older sections)

Pre-HOA-era neighborhoods in central Austin often permit STR under current rules, but deed restrictions from older subdivisions sometimes stay in force. Pull deed restriction history before closing.

Typically restricted

The typically restricted Austin submarkets

These submarkets typically restrict STR through HOA covenants, deed restrictions, or building rules regardless of city code. Exceptions exist but are case-specific. If the STR strategy is central to the investment plan, these submarkets rarely work.

Westlake master-planned

78746 subdivisions

Davenport Ranch, Rollingwood, most Rob Roy HOAs explicitly restrict STR. Deed restrictions reinforce. Luxury estate neighborhoods typically prohibit nightly rental.

Steiner Ranch

78732 and 78733

Master-planned community with HOA restrictions covering STR. Vandegrift-feeder subdivisions, Rio Vista, Lake Pointe typically prohibit nightly rental activity.

Rough Hollow

78738

Master-planned Lake Travis waterfront community with Yacht Club amenity. HOA explicitly restricts STR in current covenants despite the waterfront vacation-STR demand.

Barton Creek Lakeside

78669 gated

Gated country-club community on Lake Travis. HOA covenants restrict STR despite Hill Country vacation demand. Distinct from non-HOA Spicewood areas where STR works.

Mueller

78723 master-planned

LEED-ND Platinum master-planned community with HOA. Current HOA framework typically restricts STR activity across property types.

Cedar Park / Pflugerville newer subdivisions

78613, 78660 master-planned

Newer post-2010 master-planned subdivisions in Cedar Park and Pflugerville commonly have HOA restrictions on STR. Older non-HOA pockets within the same zip codes may work; verify by subdivision.

The six-check pre-purchase protocol for Austin STR

Six specific checks separate successful Austin STR investments from the ones that get stuck after closing with no viable operating path:

  1. Current city license eligibility for the specific address and the specific STR type (Type 1 owner-occupied, Type 2 non-owner-occupied residential, Type 3 multifamily).
  2. HOA covenants review, with explicit attention to any STR-related language in the CCRs document.
  3. Condo building rules if applicable, which are often more restrictive than city code.
  4. Deed restrictions attached to the property that may predate HOA formation and remain in force.
  5. Zoning overlay for the specific address if commercial-adjacent, mixed-use, or near zoning boundaries.
  6. Hotel Occupancy Tax (HOT) registration and operating compliance mechanics.

Austin STR neighborhoods FAQ

Common STR neighborhood questions

Which Austin neighborhoods are most STR-friendly?

Dripping Springs 78620 (Hays County ETJ), Wimberley 78676 (established vacation market), Spicewood 78669 (Lake Travis west shore non-HOA areas), Horseshoe Bay 78657 (Lake LBJ), Fredericksburg 78624 (Hill Country wine country). Within City of Austin, commercial-zoned and mixed-use parcels face fewer restrictions than residential.

What Austin neighborhoods require specific STR verification?

Most City of Austin neighborhoods: downtown 78701 condos (building HOAs vary), East Austin 78702 and 78721 (block-level), South Austin 78704 (older vs newer vary), any master-planned community (Mueller, Steiner Ranch, Rough Hollow, Barton Creek Lakeside) because HOA documents typically restrict STR regardless of city code.

What Austin neighborhoods typically restrict short-term rental?

Three categories: master-planned communities with HOAs (Westlake 78746 master-planned, Steiner Ranch 78732, Rough Hollow 78738, Barton Creek Lakeside 78669, Mueller 78723, newer Cedar Park / Round Rock / Pflugerville master-planned); downtown condo buildings with building-level STR restrictions; high-end estate neighborhoods with deed restrictions (Davenport Ranch, Rob Roy, other 78746 estate areas).

Can I do owner-occupied Airbnb anywhere in Austin?

Owner-occupied Type 1 has historically been more permitted than non-owner-occupied. Most single-family residential zones allow with proper license and HOT registration. Exceptions: HOAs explicitly prohibiting any STR regardless of occupancy, and condo buildings restricting any nightly rental. HOA document review is the primary pre-purchase verification for owner-occupied plans.

What should I check before buying an Austin STR property?

Six checks: city license eligibility for specific address and STR type; HOA covenants (CCRs) specifically for STR language; condo building rules if applicable; deed restrictions; zoning overlay for the specific address; HOT registration mechanics.
