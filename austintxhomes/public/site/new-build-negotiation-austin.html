The 10-lever framework

Running every Austin new construction transaction through all 10 levers produces measurably better outcomes than negotiating only base price.

1

Closing cost credits

Tactic:Request specific-home closing cost incentive in writing, not base community package. Standing inventory and quarter-end timing produce 20-50% larger packages than the published base.

$3K-$15KTypical range
2

Design studio allowance

Tactic:Negotiate upgrade credit in writing before design studio appointment. Specific allowance applied to structural options (which lift resale value) rather than cosmetic finishes.

$5K-$20KMid-tier builders
3

Preferred lender rate buydown

Tactic:Verify preferred lender rate vs independent lender rate before accepting the preferred-lender incentive. If preferred rate runs 0.25-0.75 percent above market, incentive value is often net negative over amortization.

4

Base price

Tactic:Least flexible lever on to-be-built. Non-zero on standing inventory, especially homes approaching 90-day sitting mark. Public builder quarter-end pressure also helps here.

$2K-$15KStanding inventory
5

Title insurance choice

Tactic:Texas law gives buyer the right to choose title insurance company. Builder's preferred title company has no buyer cost advantage. Choose independent title with strong local track record.

$1.5K-$3KBuyer right
6

Appliance package

Tactic:Negotiate appliance package upgrade (included washer/dryer, refrigerator, upgraded range) as part of the incentive stack. Builder cost on bulk-sourced appliances is well below buyer replacement cost.

$2K-$6KRetail-equivalent
7

Landscape package

Tactic:Negotiate landscape package upgrade (sod extension, additional plantings, irrigation extension, patio or deck inclusion). Costs builder significantly less than buyer post-close replacement.

$2K-$8KPackage upgrade
8

HOA transfer fee waiver

Tactic:Ask for HOA transfer and capitalization fees to be builder-paid. Often included in standard incentive package but worth verifying in writing.

$500-$2KTransfer fees
9

Inspection access

Tactic:Insist on independent inspector access during construction and before closing. Builders push back; buyer representation enforces the right. Prevents post-close defect disputes.

Extended rate lock on to-be-built

Tactic:For 10-14 month to-be-built timelines, negotiate rate lock extension at contract. Alternative: cap-and-float rate lock that locks downside while permitting upside benefit if rates fall. Specific tactic depends on current rate environment.

When to pull which lever

Specific timing produces materially different outcomes. Four high-leverage scenarios for Austin new construction negotiation.

Standing inventory at 60-90 days

Base price, closing cost credits, appliance package all become significantly more flexible. Construction loan carrying cost compounds pressure on builder.

End of quarter or end of year

Public builders (Pulte PHM, Taylor Morrison TMHC, Toll Brothers TOL, DR Horton DHI, Lennar LEN) carry earnings pressure. Close dates landing before quarter-end carry additional incentive flexibility.

New community phase opening

Pricing pressure to establish early sales and comps. Design studio allowance and base price more negotiable in opening phases than mid-phase. Risk: early phase construction quality can lag.

Slowing community sales velocity

When specific community sales pace drops below builder targets, incentive packages expand. Watching MLS absorption rates in the specific community produces timing signals.

New build negotiation FAQ

Common Austin new build negotiation questions

What can be negotiated on an Austin new construction home?

10 levers: base price (least flexible), closing cost credits, design studio allowance, preferred lender rate buydown, HOA transfer fee waiver, title insurance choice, inspection access, appliance package, landscape package, extended rate lock on to-be-built. Combined: $15K-$50K typical value for represented vs unrepresented.

How much can I save with buyer representation on an Austin new build?

$15K-$50K total typically. Sources: incremental closing cost credits ($3K-$10K), design studio allowance ($5K-$20K), rate buydown + preferred lender analysis ($5K-$30K lifetime loan cost), title choice ($1.5K-$3K), inspection coordination (dispute prevention), standing-inventory timing leverage. Builder-paid representation = zero out-of-pocket cost.

Should I use the builder's preferred title company and lender?

Default no on title (Texas gives buyer the choice; builder preferred has no cost advantage). Situational on lender (use preferred only if preferred rate plus incentive beats independent rate plus smaller incentive). Pre-contract math required.

When should I negotiate on an Austin new build versus just accept the builder package?

Negotiate on every transaction. Builder packages are starting points, not final offers. Highest leverage: standing inventory 60-90 days old, end of month/quarter/year (public builder earnings pressure), new community phase openings, slowing community sales velocity.
