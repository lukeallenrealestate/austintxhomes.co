What is actually in Austin

Separate metro, 200 miles south of Westlake TX

Covers central Texas client-facing financial advisor presence and some corporate roles that have moved to Austin following the broader tech talent pattern.

Why the Westlake-TX vs Westlake-Hills confusion matters

Charles Schwab's 2020-2021 HQ relocation from San Francisco to Westlake, Texas created significant inbound Texas Schwab employee movement, but the destination Westlake is specifically Westlake, Texas, a town in Tarrant and Denton Counties of the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex, 30 miles northwest of downtown Dallas and 190 miles north of Austin. Westlake Hills is a different place: a Travis County city within the Austin metro, specifically the Eanes ISD submarket on the west side of Austin.

Google-search research for "Schwab Westlake" returns results for both cities with no clear disambiguation. Relocation guides frequently conflate the two. Schwab employees whose role is at the Westlake TX HQ sometimes end up researching Westlake Hills Austin real estate by mistake, and vice versa. The practical implication: before engaging any realtor or starting any home search, confirm with the Schwab offer which city your role is physically located in.

The Austin Schwab cohort workforce profile

The Austin Schwab workforce profile differs from the Westlake TX HQ workforce in substantive ways. Austin Schwab roles concentrate in client-facing branch functions (financial consultant, portfolio consultant), Schwab Advisor Services relationship-management roles serving the Austin-area Registered Investment Advisor firm ecosystem, trading-services roles that can be located flexibly, and some technology and operations roles that have moved to Austin following the broader Austin tech talent pattern.

Compensation structure for client-facing Austin Schwab roles typically runs base plus bonus plus Schwab (SCHW) equity, with Series 7, Series 66, and often CFP credentials required. Buying power bands for the Austin Schwab client-facing cohort typically run $400K-$900K for entry financial consultant, $800K-$1.5M for senior consultant and portfolio consultant, and $1.5M-$3M+ for Schwab Advisor Services leadership and senior relationship manager roles.
