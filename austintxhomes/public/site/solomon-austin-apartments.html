The Building

East Austin
Smart Living

Solomon sits at 1414 East 51st Street, directly across from the Mueller master-planned community. This is East Austin's sweet spot: walkable to Mueller's HEB, Alamo Drafthouse, and Lake Park, but priced meaningfully below Mueller's own condos and townhomes.

The floor plan mix is unusual for the area. Solomon offers studios, 1 bedrooms, 2 bedrooms, plus a rare set of street-level live/work residences with a ground-floor commercial-ready space and residential above. If you're a small business owner or a remote worker who wants a physical presence without a separate lease, the live/work units are worth touring.

Every unit includes Bluetooth-enabled entry locks, smart thermostats, Wi-Fi smart lights, USB ports throughout, top-performing appliances, and built-in serving bars with wine fridges. Solomon is professionally managed by Greystar, one of the largest apartment operators in the country.

FAQ

Solomon Austin
Questions Answered

Where exactly is Solomon located?
1414 East 51st Street, Austin TX 78723, in East Austin adjacent to Mueller. About 12 minutes to downtown, 15 to The Domain, 5 minutes on foot to Mueller's HEB and Lake Park.
What floor plan types are available?
Studios, 1BR, 2BR, and street-level live/work residences. The live/work units are rare in East Austin and worth a look if you run a small business.
Is Solomon pet-friendly?
Yes. 2 pets per apartment, $300 non-refundable fee per pet, $25 to $30 monthly pet management fee, $25 monthly pet rent per pet. No weight limit; breed restrictions apply.
Who manages Solomon?
Greystar, one of the largest apartment operators in the country. Standardized lease terms, professional maintenance, resident portal.
Should I rent at Solomon or buy in 78723?
Depends on your timeline. If you're staying 3+ years, buying nearby (Mueller condos, East Austin bungalows) often beats renting. If you're 1 to 2 years or need max flexibility, renting at Solomon is the safer play. I'll run the actual numbers with you.
How close is Solomon to Mueller?
Directly across the street. About 5 minutes on foot to Mueller Lake Park, HEB, Alamo Drafthouse, and the shops at Mueller. Same lifestyle, meaningfully lower rent.
