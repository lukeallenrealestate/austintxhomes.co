Luke's Take on Old West Austin

The single most valuable piece of hyper-local knowledge in Old West Austin is the school catchment boundary. Casis Elementary vs Mathews Elementary can differ across a single street, and the value implications are meaningful. Casis is significantly higher-rated on state metrics and drives a real premium for family buyers. Getting the exact school assignment right before you make an offer (or before you list) is a $75K-$150K decision.

Historic overlay status matters too. Roughly half of Clarksville and portions of Old Enfield sit under Historic Landmark District protection. Any property in Old West Austin should be individually verified for overlay status before you plan any renovation, addition, or teardown.
