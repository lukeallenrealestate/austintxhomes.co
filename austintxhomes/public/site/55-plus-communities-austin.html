How to pick between these communities

Three questions narrow the choice efficiently. First: how deep do you want the active adult community experience? Sun City Georgetown offers the deepest programming (dozens of clubs, three amenity centers, three golf courses, on-site retail). Heritage at Vizcaya offers meaningful but smaller programming. Travisso 55-plus section is a smaller slice within a larger mixed-age community. Horseshoe Bay is a lake resort community rather than a programmed active adult community.

Second: how important is central Austin proximity? Heritage at Vizcaya and Travisso 55-plus run the shortest commute to central Austin (25-40 minutes). Sun City Georgetown runs longer (35-45 minutes). Horseshoe Bay is a destination choice (55 minutes) rather than a daily-commute community.

Third: what is your buying band? Sun City Georgetown and Heritage at Vizcaya average in the $500K range with inventory starting around $350K-$400K. Horseshoe Bay averages higher ($756K) reflecting lake-adjacent inventory. Each community has entry-tier and premium-tier inventory producing different specific property matches.

55-plus communities FAQ

Common 55-plus community questions

What is the biggest 55-plus community in the Austin area?

Sun City Georgetown. Del Webb development, 9,000+ homes across three phases. Three golf courses, three amenity centers, dozens of resident clubs, on-site shopping and dining, purpose-built age-appropriate infrastructure. Georgetown 78633. 458 active listings between $350K and $900K averaging $535K.

How does Heritage at Vizcaya compare to Sun City Georgetown?

Heritage substantially smaller (~1,000 homes vs 9,000+), single golf course (vs three), single amenity center (vs three), limited clubs (vs extensive programming), closer-in to central Austin (25-30 min vs 35-45), roughly comparable average price ($522K vs $535K). Choice typically between community-depth preference (Sun City) and closer-in location (Heritage).

Is Horseshoe Bay a 55-plus community?

Not formally age-restricted but demographically skews heavily to active adult retirees. Resort community on Lake LBJ with Horseshoe Bay Resort amenities (three golf courses, marina, lake access, dining). Primary Austin-area destination for lake-adjacent retirement. 55 minutes NW of Austin. 181 active listings averaging $756K.

What are the HOA fees typical in Austin 55-plus communities?

Higher than general-market HOA because they fund age-restricted amenity packages. Sun City Georgetown historically around $150-$200 per month (verify current). Heritage at Vizcaya similar range. Horseshoe Bay varies by subdivision with separate club memberships for golf and resort. Travisso 55-plus section lower reflecting smaller amenity package.
