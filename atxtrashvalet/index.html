How Trash Valet Works

A seamless experience for residents and property managers — no heavy lifting, no long walks to the dumpster.

1

Residents Set Out Trash

Each night, residents place their trash bag in the provided doorstep container or bag holder outside their unit door before the nightly cutoff time (typically 8 PM).

2

Our Crew Collects It

Our uniformed, professional crew walks every hallway and breezeway, collecting bags from each doorstep — every scheduled night, rain or shine.

3

Delivered to the Dumpster

All collected trash is transported directly to the property's designated dumpster enclosure. Walkways stay clean. Residents stay happy. You look great.

Why It Matters

Why Property Managers Love Trash Valet

Trash valet isn't just a convenience — it's a retention and revenue tool that pays for itself.

Boost Resident Retention

Studies show trash valet is one of the top 3 amenities that influence lease renewals. Residents who don't have to haul trash are happier residents who stay longer.

Reduce Dumpster Overflow

When trash is collected nightly, it distributes evenly across pickup cycles — reducing compactor overloads, overflow complaints, and costly emergency hauls.

Eliminate Pest Pressure

Trash left in hallways overnight attracts roaches, rodents, and other pests. Nightly doorstep collection removes the food source before it becomes a problem.

Improve Curb Appeal

Clean breezeways and hallways make a strong first impression on prospective residents touring your property — and on Google review writers.

Justify Higher Rents

Trash valet is a premium amenity. Most properties charge residents $25–$35/month for the service, creating a modest revenue stream that offsets the program cost.

Liability Reduction

Overfull dumpster areas and hallway trash create trip hazards. Nightly valet service keeps common areas clear, reducing your property's liability exposure.
